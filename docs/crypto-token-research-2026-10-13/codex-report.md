# Allworld: crypto currency, real value, and the regulatory cost

**Research date: 13 October 2026. Verdict: no-go for the proposal as stated.**

**Using a cryptocurrency instead of fiat does not avoid financial regulation.** A token that players can trade, hold for appreciation, and later sell outside Allworld can introduce securities, exchange, custody, money-transmission, AML, sanctions, tax, and gambling obligations. Announcing the future listing can matter before external trading begins.

There is a viable alternative: keep the simulation currency closed-loop, and pay eligible creators or service providers from actual revenue through appropriately licensed partners.

The existing real-value plan already prohibits cashable tokens and favours verified businesses, skills, gigs, and revenue-funded creator payouts. That direction is better supported by the evidence than the proposed token.

## 1. What changes when game money becomes real value?

The proposed sequence is:

1. Create an Allworld token.
2. Allow players worldwide to trade it inside the game.
3. Publish transactions.
4. Later introduce exchange or DEX trading.

Regulators examine the entire arrangement: what holders receive, what the founder promises, who controls supply and balances, and who facilitates transactions.

| Design choice | Regulatory consequence to examine |
|---|---|
| Sell tokens to finance game development | Public offering and securities analysis |
| Promise external listings, appreciation, buybacks, staking, or revenue sharing | Investment-contract and financial-promotion analysis |
| Match players’ token buy/sell orders | Exchange or trading-platform regulation |
| Hold private keys or control withdrawals | Custody and potentially money transmission |
| Transfer value between players or currencies | VASP, payment, AML and sanctions obligations |
| Promise redemption at a fixed naira or dollar value | Payment, stored-value, e-money or stablecoin regulation |
| Let players stake valuable tokens on random outcomes | Gambling analysis |
| Award tokens for work, promotion or play | Income, valuation and reporting questions |
| Publish identifiable transaction histories | Data-protection obligations, especially for minors |

These are overlapping questions. Passing the securities analysis does not settle the payments analysis. Avoiding custody does not settle token-offering or marketing rules.

A public ledger helps reconstruct transactions. It does not establish customers’ identities, detect every beneficial-owner relationship, authorize prohibited transfers, or replace a licence. FATF’s **21 July 2026** DeFi report expressly considers arrangements where identifiable people retain control or sufficient influence, despite decentralization claims. [FATF, 2026](https://www.fatf-gafi.org/en/news/targeted-report-decentralised-finance-2026.html)

**Application to Allworld:** the founder would control the game, reward rules, token utility and likely the launch. Calling the currency “decentralized” would not remove those facts.

## 2. Financial regulation by jurisdiction

### Nigeria

#### Securities and token issuance

The **Investments and Securities Act 2025**, section 357, expressly includes virtual and digital assets in its definition of securities. Its Second Schedule also includes virtual assets, digital assets and DLT offers, tokens and products. The exchange definition includes facilities bringing together buyers and sellers and matching transactions in virtual assets. [ISA 2025, official SEC text](https://www.sec.gov.ng/documents/1319/Investments_and_Securities_Act_2025_x9rSXtI.pdf)

This makes the “it is only a game token” argument particularly weak in Nigeria. It does not mean every ordinary game score is automatically a regulated security; Allworld needs a written classification of its actual rights, transferability, offering and services.

The SEC’s **11 May 2022 Digital Asset Rules**, still published in its current rule repository, provide initial-offering assessment and white-paper requirements, alongside separate rules for offering platforms, custodians, VASPs and exchanges. Their VASP definition covers fiat/crypto exchange, crypto/crypto exchange, transfers, custody and financial services connected with issuance. [SEC Digital Asset Rules, 2022](https://www.sec.gov.ng/documents/8/Rules-on-Issuance-Offering-and-Custody-of-Digital-Assets.pdf)

**Application:** Allworld could perform several regulated functions simultaneously. Issuing a token, running its internal market and controlling players’ balances should not be budgeted as a single ordinary software activity.

#### ARIP is supervised entry, not an exemption

SEC’s Accelerated Regulatory Incubation Programme provides conditional admission and supervision. Its eligibility guidance requires Nigerian incorporation, a physical office and a resident chief executive. [SEC programme guidance, 24 May 2025](https://www.sec.gov.ng/fintech-and-innovation-hub-finport/finport-programs-ri-and-arip/)

A **3 July 2026** SEC announcement explicitly states that Approval-in-Principle **is not a final licence**. Participation remains conditional and limited to the approved scope. [SEC ARIP announcement, 2026](https://sec.gov.ng/for-investors/keep-track-of-circulars/sec-clears-further-2-additional-vasps-for-admission-into-the-accelerated-regulatory-incubation-programme-arip/)

#### The capital requirements are substantial

SEC’s **16 January 2026** circular specifies:

| Potential regulated function | Revised minimum capital |
|---|---:|
| Ancillary VASP | ₦300 million |
| Digital asset intermediary | ₦500 million |
| Digital asset platform operator, including token issuers | ₦500 million |
| Digital asset offering platform | ₦1 billion |
| Digital asset exchange | ₦2 billion |
| Digital asset custodian | ₦2 billion |

[SEC capital circular, 16 January 2026](https://www.sec.gov.ng/for-investors/keep-track-of-circulars/revised-minimum-capital-mc-for-regulated-capital-market-entities/)

The **18 March 2026** implementation guidance makes revised capital a prerequisite for new registrations from 16 January 2026. The June 2027 transition is for existing operators and specified pending applicants. It also requires compliance with the capital requirement for each regulated function performed. **The ₦2 billion figures are capital requirements, not application fees.** [SEC capital implementation guidance, 2026](https://sec.gov.ng/documents/1445/Guidelines_on_New_Capital_Base_CMOS_2026.pdf)

The applicable category, possible accumulation of requirements and any approved sandbox relief need SEC confirmation.

#### CBN: regulated banking access does not mean unrestricted crypto payments

CBN’s **22 December 2023** guidelines replaced its earlier banking restrictions and permit regulated banking relationships with VASPs. Banks remain prohibited from holding or trading virtual currencies on their own account. The circular also recognizes VASPs within Nigeria’s money-laundering framework. [CBN VASP banking guidelines](https://www.cbn.gov.ng/out/2024/fprd/guidelines%20on%20operations%20of%20bank%20accounts%20for%20virtual%20asset%20providers.pdf)

A significant recent development is the **July 2026 Presidential Executive Order on Virtual Assets Coordination**. The State House describes securities activities as SEC-regulated, and non-security virtual-asset payment, settlement, custody and related services as CBN-regulated. It preserves agencies’ statutory mandates and introduces coordination and supervised testing. [State House announcement, published 18 July 2026](https://statehouse.gov.ng/president-tinubu-signs-executive-order-on-virtual-assets-establishes-council-to-harmonise-regulation-of-digital-economy/)

**Application:** even a successful argument that Allworld’s token is non-security does not establish that its payment and custody services are unregulated. Counsel must reconcile the ISA, SEC rules and the current coordinated framework.

An August 2026 replacement digital-asset rules proposal was confirmed through the industry association’s consultation response, but the official draft could not be retrieved successfully. Its final adoption and detailed requirements are **[unverified]**. Do not treat reported proposed fees as enacted requirements. [VASPA consultation response, 5 September 2026](https://vaspa.org/vaspa-formal-commentary-sec-proposed-digital-and-virtual-asset-rules/)

- **Binance:** SEC’s **July 2023** notice identified Binance’s Nigerian solicitation as unregistered and directed it to stop. EFCC subsequently confirms investigating Binance. The US State Department confirms Tigran Gambaryan’s release on medical grounds on **24 October 2024**. That release is not proof that Binance’s corporate legal problems disappeared. The latest Nigerian corporate court disposition as of this report is **[unverified]**. [SEC notice](https://sec.gov.ng/for-investors/keep-track-of-circulars/warning-against-investing-with-binance/), [EFCC account](https://efcc.gov.ng/news/olukoyede-advocates-elaborate-reforms-to-reduce-foreign-investment-risks-in-africa), [State Department release](https://2021-2025.state.gov/release-of-u-s-citizen-tigran-gambaryan/)

### United States

#### Howey still matters, with important 2026 guidance

The Supreme Court’s **27 May 1946 Howey decision** established the investment-contract analysis: investment in a common enterprise with expected profits derived from others’ efforts. [Official US Reports](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/USREPORTS-328/USREPORTS-328-293)

The SEC’s **17 March 2026 interpretation** superseded its 2019 digital-asset framework. It distinguishes non-security crypto assets from investment contracts involving those assets. Functional tools and collectibles can be non-securities, while their sale with relevant managerial promises can still be a securities transaction. [SEC interpretation, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/interp/2026/33-11412.pdf)

**Application:** “collect game money now; we will develop the economy and arrange external trading later” invites scrutiny of the founder’s promises and holders’ profit expectations. This is a risk assessment, not a finding that every Allworld token distribution necessarily satisfies Howey. Free distributions require their own analysis.

The SEC’s **18 August 2026 Regulation Crypto Assets** offering exemptions and transition safe harbour were **proposed**, not a general live permission to launch. [SEC proposal announcement](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026-76-sec-proposes-new-regulation-crypto-assets)

#### FinCEN money transmission

FinCEN’s **18 March 2013** guidance expressly addresses convertible virtual currency. Ordinary users purchasing goods or services are generally not money-services businesses merely because they use it. Business administrators and exchangers can be money transmitters. [FinCEN, 2013](https://www.fincen.gov/resources/statutes-regulations/guidance/application-fincens-regulations-persons-administering)

Its **9 May 2019** guidance applies this framework to custody, trading, token issuance and decentralized applications. Required compliance can include MSB registration, an AML programme, records and suspicious-activity reporting. Pure software development differs from operating a transfer service; fundraising alone does not automatically constitute money transmission. [FinCEN, 2019](https://www.fincen.gov/resources/statutes-regulations/guidance/application-fincens-regulations-certain-business-models)

A Nigerian company serving substantial US business cannot assume its foreign incorporation removes these requirements.

#### State laws

- **New York:** BitLicense rules cover relevant issuance/administration, custody, exchange and transmission involving New York residents. Federal MSB registration does not replace state authorization. [NYDFS licensing guidance, checked October 2026](https://www.dfs.ny.gov/apps_and_licensing/virtual_currency_businesses)
- **California:** DFAL licensing began **1 July 2026**. The regulator distinguishes value restricted to a publisher’s game ecosystem from covered digital financial assets. External cash conversion weakens the gaming exclusion. The FAQ’s indexed text was verified, but direct retrieval failed. [DFPI FAQ](https://dfpi.ca.gov/regulated-industries/digital-financial-assets/digital-financial-assets-law-frequently-asked-questions/)

A nationwide launch requires a state-by-state assessment, not just FinCEN registration.

Treasury’s **30 September 2026** rule expects **18 January 2027**. | A payment-stablecoin framework with issuer, reserve, redemption and compliance requirements. It does not exempt an appreciating game token. |

[GENIUS enacted statute](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-119publ27/pdf/PLAW-119publ27.pdf), [Treasury implementation status](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-09-30/html/2026-19966.htm), [Senate CLARITY statement](https://www.banking.senate.gov/newsroom/majority/scott-statement-on-clarity-act-vote)

### European Union

MiCA’s general crypto-asset regime has applied since **30 December 2024**, with stablecoin provisions applying earlier. It distinguishes ordinary crypto assets, asset-referenced tokens and e-money tokens. Financial instruments fall under existing securities legislation instead. [MiCA, Regulation 2023/1114](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/FR-EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32023R1114)

For a non-exempt public offer of an ordinary token, expect legal-person, white-paper, notification, publication and marketing obligations. White-paper notification is generally at least **20 working days before publication**, rather than general prior approval. [MiCA article 8](https://www.esma.europa.eu/publications-and-data/interactive-single-rulebook/mica/article-8-notification-crypto-asset-white)

#### The closed-loop exemption conflicts with the founder’s promise

Article 4 provides exemptions for specified free distributions, existing utility services and contractual limited merchant networks. “Free” excludes exchanges for personal data or other monetary/nonmonetary benefits. Limited-network offers exceeding **€1 million over 12 months** require notification.

**Article 4(4) removes the relevant offer exemptions when the issuer communicates an intention to seek admission to trading.** Announcing an exchange or DEX release can therefore defeat the exemption before that release. The related custody/transfer exemption also has limits where non-exempt offers or trading admission exist. [MiCA article 4, official ESMA reproduction](https://www.esma.europa.eu/publications-and-data/interactive-single-rulebook/mica/article-4-offers-public-crypto-assets-other)

#### Issuance and service licensing are separate

Operating covered custody, exchange or trading services generally requires CASP authorization or an eligible regulated-institution route. [MiCA article 59](https://www.esma.europa.eu/publications-and-data/interactive-single-rulebook/mica/article-59-authorisation)

The maximum grandfathering period ended **1 July 2026**; it is unavailable to a new Allworld launch. [ESMA statement, April 2026](https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2026-04/ESMA75-113276571-1679_Statement_on_the_end_of_transitional_periods_under_MiCA.pdf)

A token referencing one official currency raises e-money-token requirements, ordinarily involving an authorized credit or e-money institution. [MiCA article 48](https://www.esma.europa.eu/publications-and-data/interactive-single-rulebook/mica/article-48-requirements-offer-public-or)

NFTs are not a blanket escape. Genuine uniqueness matters; interchangeable collections, fractionalization and security-like rights require separate analysis. [ESMA final classification report, December 2024](https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2024-12/ESMA75453128700-1323_Final_Report_Guidelines_on_the_conditions_and_criteria_for_the_qualification_of_CAs_as_FIs.pdf)

### United Kingdom

Three layers matter:

1. **Financial promotions now:** since **8 October 2023**, qualifying crypto promotions to UK consumers require a lawful communication route, including when sent by overseas firms. Relevant rules include risk warnings, cooling-off, categorization and appropriateness checks. Unauthorized promotions can constitute a criminal offence. [FCA guidance, updated 6 February 2026](https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/cryptoassets/marketing-uk-consumers)
2. **AML registration now:** covered exchange and custodian businesses carrying on business in the UK must register before starting. Registration is not FCA endorsement. [FCA guidance, updated 12 February 2026](https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/financial-crime/money-laundering-terrorist-financing/cryptoassets-aml-ctf-regime)
3. **Broader authorization later:** the enacted 2026 cryptoasset regulations introduce the new regime from **25 October 2027**. [FCA preparation guidance, updated 16 September 2026](https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/new-regime-cryptoasset-regulation/what-you-need-to-do)

The new limited-network exclusion has restricted transfer/use conditions. Broad player trading and exchange listing cannot casually claim it, and exclusion from one regime does not automatically remove AML obligations. [FCA perimeter guidance, September 2026](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/perg18)

### Issuer obligations versus player obligations

| Party | What can trigger obligations |
|---|---|
| Allworld/token issuer | Offering, marketing, redemption promises, revenue sharing, treasury sales, custody, transfers, matching orders and regulated payment services |
| Ordinary player | Identity checks imposed by providers; income and disposal taxes; applicable sanctions prohibitions; age and geographic eligibility |
| Player running a business | Exchanging, transferring or safeguarding value for others can introduce licensing/AML duties beyond personal use |
| Creator or “scholar” | Earnings may be income; later token sales or spending may create separate taxable disposals; contractual and employment questions remain |

**AML/KYC:** Nigeria’s SEC rules require customer identification, beneficial-owner assessment, monitoring and records, with ultimate identification responsibility remaining with the operator even when a third party is used. [SEC AML regulations, 12 May 2022](https://www.sec.gov.ng/documents/7/SEC-AMLCFTCPF-REGULATIONS-12-MAY-2022.pdf) EU transfer-information rules have applied since **30 December 2024**. [EBA Travel Rule guidance, 4 July 2024](https://www.eba.europa.eu/publications-and-media/press-releases/eba-issues-travel-rule-guidance-tackle-money-laundering-and-terrorist-financing-transfers-funds-and)

**Sanctions:** Nigerian regulated entities face screening, freezing and reporting duties, illustrated by SEC’s **August 2026** sanctions circular. OFAC restrictions apply to persons and transactions within US jurisdiction regardless of whether payment uses crypto. [SEC sanctions circular](https://www.sec.gov.ng/for-investors/keep-track-of-circulars/designation-of-individuals-and-entities-by-the-nigeria-sanctions-committee-nsc/), [OFAC virtual-currency guidance, 15 October 2021](https://ofac.treasury.gov/recent-actions/20211015)

**Tax:** Nigeria’s **31 July 2026** guidance expressly includes gaming utility tokens, gains on disposal, income receipts, reporting and valuation. It specifies **7.5% VAT on VASP service fees** and **1.5% stamp duty for covered token/fiat acquisitions**, with category-specific collection rules. Do not apply those percentages indiscriminately to every game balance or transaction. [NRS virtual-asset tax guidance](https://www.nrs.gov.ng/uploads/Guidelines_on_taxation_of_Virtual_Assets_31_7_26_7cd2ef8dab.pdf)

US digital assets are generally property for tax purposes; service receipts and subsequent disposals can have separate consequences. EU DAC8 and UK CARF introduce reporting requirements from **2026**, alongside national taxes. [IRS FAQs, December 2025](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/frequently-asked-questions-on-digital-asset-transactions), [European Commission DAC8](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/taxation/tax-transparency-cooperation/administrative-co-operation-and-mutual-assistance/directive-administrative-cooperation-dac/dac8_en?prefLang=lt), [HMRC, updated 1 January 2026](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/check-if-youll-need-to-report-cryptoasset-data-to-hmrc)

**No cash withdrawal does not necessarily mean no tax event.**

## 3. Gambling, loot boxes and play-to-earn

**Cash convertibility does not automatically turn every game mechanic into gambling.** It can, however, make the stake or prize legally valuable.

The dangerous combination is usually consideration or a stake, chance, and a valuable prize. Jurisdictions differ, and some regulate skill competitions or promotional prizes too.

| Mechanic | Assessment for Allworld |
|---|---|
| Buy a specified cosmetic at a known price | Lower gambling risk |
| Pay valuable tokens for a random loot box | High-risk gambling analysis |
| Stake tokens on a lottery, casino or simulated event | Strong gambling indicators |
| Pay tournament entry fees for valuable prizes | Skill/chance and local competition-law analysis |
| Receive deterministic creator compensation | Different from wagering |
| Receive a random reward during genuinely free play | Not automatically gambling everywhere |
| Convert points into gift cards | Gift cards remain valuable prizes |
| Pay for randomized tradable NFT breeding or drops | Requires specific review |

### Nigeria: state jurisdiction matters

The Supreme Court’s **22 November 2024** decision in **SC/1/2008** treated lottery regulation as a residual state matter. Lagos’s regulator confirms the judgment and subsequent state coordination. [LSLGA/FSGRN statement, November 2024](https://lslga.org/federation-of-state-gaming-regulators-of-nigeria-fsgrn-meets-to-discuss-supreme-court-judgment-affirming-state-jurisdiction-over-gaming-regulation/)

The reported FCT exception preserving federal lottery authority in Abuja is **[unverified against the certified judgment]** in this research.

Lagos’s **2021** legislation and regulator guidance cover categories including online/remote gaming and promotional competitions. Under-18 betting is prohibited. This does not establish that every ordinary video game needs a gambling licence; it means the exact valuable-prize mechanics need classification. [Legislative summary](https://lslga.org/legislation/), [licensing categories](https://lslga.org/licensing-categories-and-compliance/), [underage notice](https://lslga.org/lagos-state-lotteries-board-prohibits-underage-gaming/)

**Players’ actual locations matter.** A player standing in virtual Kano may physically be in Lagos, London or New York. The game map is not a licensing boundary. Counsel must assess Lagos, Oyo, Rivers, Kano and FCT treatment where applicable.

### Belgium and the Netherlands are different

- **Belgium:** the 2018 findings covered paid boxes in FIFA, Overwatch and CS:GO. A **30 June 2025** government release confirms paid loot boxes remain prohibited. Cash-out is not a universal prerequisite for Belgian liability. [2018 ministerial release](https://www.koengeens.be/news/2018/04/25/loot-boxen-in-drie-videogames-in-strijd-met-kansspelwetgeving), [2025 government release](https://news.economie.fgov.be/251680-huit-jeux-controles-sur-dix-manipulent-les-joueurs/)
- **Netherlands:** on **9 March 2022**, the highest administrative court overturned the FIFA penalty because the packs formed part of the broader skill game rather than a separate game of chance. That fact-specific ruling does not authorize standalone token lotteries. [Court ruling announcement](https://www.raadvanstate.nl/@130206/dwangsom-onterecht-opgelegd-loot-boxes/)

### UK and US examples

UK Gambling Commission guidance treats tradable or convertible game items as money or money’s worth when used for gambling. [Guidance updated 5 July 2021](https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/licensees-and-businesses/guide/page/digital-and-virtual-currencies)

On **6 June 2025**, New York’s attorney general announced that **26 sweepstakes casinos** stopped selling sweepstakes coins after enforcement. Their coins were redeemable for cash and prizes, including gift cards. [NY enforcement release](https://ag.ny.gov/press-release/2025/attorney-general-james-stops-illegal-online-sweepstakes-casinos)

A legitimate no-purchase route can affect sweepstakes analysis, but “dual coins,” “free entries” and gift cards do not provide a nationwide safe harbour.

**Recommendation:** retain the repo’s prohibition on paid chance mechanics. Keep minors outside any real-value financial pilot.

## 6. Why the token economy is likely to fail

The following is economic analysis supported by the documented examples, rather than a legal finding about any particular issuer.

### Printing rewards does not fund withdrawals

A token reward creates an asset someone may try to sell. It does not create a buyer with outside money.

For durable payouts, the system ultimately needs genuine entertainment spending, advertising, purchases of useful services or other external revenue. New investors buying because they expect later buyers are not a dependable source of operating income.

### Allworld’s existing simulation would become a financial faucet

`BUSINESS.md` says simulated passers-by create new in-game naira. It reports career and stall earnings, caps and sinks suitable for balancing a life-sim.

Those passers-by do not bring real revenue.

If simulated earnings become redeemable:

- Every reward rule becomes a potential withdrawal obligation or source of token selling.
- Every exploit becomes financially valuable.
- Bots can harvest bounded rewards across many accounts.
- Balance changes can impose real losses on players.
- Legacy accumulated balances create launch supply.

**Do not convert existing balances into a cashable asset by default.** The simulation was designed around fictional purchasing power, not funded financial claims.

### Common failure mechanisms

| Mechanism | Why it damages the economy |
|---|---|
| Reward inflation | Issuance outpaces consumption; Axie documented a 4:1 imbalance |
| “Sinks” that buy more earning capacity | Demand depends on expected future returns and new entrants |
| Mercenary users | Participation falls when payout yields fall |
| Bots and account farms | Automated extraction consumes rewards intended for people |
| Launch speculation | Early balances and insider unlocks can overwhelm real purchase demand |
| Wash trading | Transaction volume exaggerates independent demand |
| Buybacks funded by token sales | Uses proceeds from new buyers to support existing holders, rather than solving revenue weakness |
| Fixed supply | Limits issuance but does not create utility, buyers or liquidity |

Actual manipulation enforcement exists. On **13 June 2025**, DOJ announced Gotbit’s criminal resolution, approximately **$23 million forfeiture**, and its founder’s prison sentence for fraudulent volume manipulation. [DOJ Gotbit outcome](https://www.justice.gov/usao-ma/pr/cryptocurrency-financial-services-firm-gotbit-and-founder-sentenced-market-manipulation)

Do not label every falling token a pump-and-dump. Equally, do not confuse a public ledger with protection from one.

**The more durable designs sell entertainment or useful creations, restrict financial extraction, and fund payouts from realized revenue.** Measure human retention, paying customers, revenue net of incentives and fraud losses. Wallets and transaction volume are insufficient.

## 7. Security and operating costs

| Risk | Required response |
|---|---|
| Smart-contract defects | Independent review, controlled permissions, upgrade policy and incident procedures |
| Admin-key compromise | Separated signing authority, secure key storage, access reviews and withdrawal controls |
| Bridges | Avoid unnecessary bridging; assess validator and asset-backing dependencies |
| Custody | Licensed provider where required, asset segregation, reconciliation and recovery arrangements |
| Wallet phishing | Clear signing flows, restricted approvals, account recovery and support |
| Game exploits | Server-authoritative financial actions, replay protection, fraud review and payout holds |
| Insider trading/manipulation | Allocation disclosure, vesting, conflict rules and transaction surveillance |
| Exchange/partner failure | Counterparty diligence, concentration limits and continuity planning |
| Public transaction histories | Privacy assessment and separation of identity records from public data |

Ronin is the game-specific warning. The FBI also attributed approximately **$1.5 billion** stolen from Bybit on **21 February 2025** to North Korea. Large professional operators remain vulnerable. [FBI notice, February 2025](https://www.fbi.gov/investigate/cyber/alerts/2025/north-korea-responsible-for-1-5-billion-bybit-hack)

### KYC is only one component of compliance cost

A regulated operation needs staff to handle rejected verification, sanctions matches, suspicious transactions, appeals, fraud, reconciliation, tax reporting and incidents.

An archived **November 2024** Sumsub plan advertised $1.85 per compliance verification, but this is a historical benchmark, not a current Nigerian crypto-service quote. Current all-in provider pricing and acceptance are **[unverified]**. [Historical pricing](https://sumsub.com/pricing-old/1nov2024/), [current pricing page](https://sumsub.com/pricing/)

For planning, assuming **$2–$5 per onboarding** means $20,000–$50,000 for 10,000 users before staff, blockchain monitoring, Travel Rule services and other charges. **[unverified budget assumption]**

### Chain choice does not solve the regulatory problem

- **Ethereum:** fees vary with execution and congestion; L2s can reduce costs but introduce additional infrastructure dependencies. [Ethereum documentation, updated June 2026](https://ethereum.org/developers/docs/gas/)
- **Solana:** the documented base fee is **5,000 lamports per signature**, plus priority fees. One million single-signature transactions would consume **5 SOL in base fees alone**, excluding account storage, additional signatures, priority fees and infrastructure. [Solana fee documentation, checked October 2026](https://solana.com/docs/core/fees)
- **Private chain:** requires operating infrastructure and governance. It does not create liquidity or a legal exemption.

For Allworld, frequent simulation transactions are better kept off-chain. If a justified on-chain asset is introduced later, choose one supported chain and minimize bridges.

### The public-ledger proposal creates a privacy problem

Publishing students’ names or linkable wallet histories can reveal holdings and behaviour indefinitely. Nigerian child-consent and privacy requirements apply; EU blockchain guidance also addresses minimization and erasure difficulties. [NDPA 2023, section 31](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Nigeria_Data_Protection_Act_2023.pdf), [EDPB final blockchain guidelines, 7 July 2026](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/system/files/2026-07/edpb_guidelines_202502_blockchain_v2_en.pdf)

Keep identity documents and KYC data off-chain. Pseudonymous addresses can still be personal data when linkable to people.

## 9. Verdict, budget, timeline and legal questions

### Decision

**No-go:** a globally tradable proprietary currency whose purpose is avoiding financial regulation.

**Go:** closed-loop gameplay, verified commerce discovery, learning and skills.

**Go-only-if:** revenue-funded creator payouts through properly scoped licensed partners.

Reconsider a custom token only when there is a specific customer benefit that requires it, an established paying audience, and enough capital to operate a financial product.

For a custom-token project, separately fund the applicable Nigerian capital requirements. Operating an issuer, exchange and custodian could create several requirements; counsel and SEC must settle the classification and treatment. Partnership may reduce functions Allworld performs, but does not guarantee elimination of issuer obligations.

A Nigeria-only pilot is financially and operationally more credible than “anyone anywhere.” Regulatory approval timing is not guaranteed.

### Conditions before a real-value pilot

1. Written classification of the exact token, payout and transaction flows.
2. Confirmed licences or specific regulatory authorization for every regulated function.
3. Written partner and processor acceptance of the business model.
4. Adult-only financial pilot with identity and geographic eligibility checks.
5. No paid random rewards, wagers, investment returns or guaranteed earnings.
6. A funded payout pool that survives without new token purchasers.
7. Fraud, sanctions, reconciliation, incident and tax processes staffed before launch.
8. Clear player rights, refunds, complaints and shutdown arrangements.
9. Evidence that people retain interest when financial incentives stop.

### Questions a Nigerian fintech/securities lawyer must answer

Ask for a written opinion tied to diagrams of the proposed flows.

1. Under ISA 2025 section 357 and current rules, how is each proposed asset classified?
2. Does announcing future cash conversion or listings make the initial distribution an offering or regulated promotion?
3. Which functions would Allworld perform: issuer, DAPO, intermediary, exchange, offering platform, custodian, payment provider or another category?
4. How do the July 2026 coordination order, CBN framework and any finalized replacement SEC rules affect that classification?
5. Which capital, fidelity-bond, fees and staffing requirements apply? Can approved incubation provide relief?
6. What exactly can each proposed partner’s current licence or AIP cover?
7. Who controls assets, keys, withdrawal decisions, escrow and settlement? What happens if the partner fails?
8. Which AML/KYC, beneficial-owner, monitoring, sanctions, Travel Rule and NFIU reporting duties remain with Allworld?
9. What Nigerian tax treatment applies to purchases, earned rewards, swaps, gifts, cosmetic spending, creator compensation and treasury sales?
10. What must be collected, withheld, reported and retained under the 2026 tax guidance?
11. Which mechanics need state gaming or promotional-competition approval, including Lagos, Oyo, Rivers, Kano and FCT?
12. How should minors be excluded from financial functions, and what parental-consent and privacy rules apply elsewhere?
13. Can any transaction ledger be public while meeting NDPA obligations? Which data must remain private?
14. What processor approvals, consumer disclosures, refund rules and advertising approvals are needed?
15. Which foreign markets must be blocked until their securities, payment, promotions and gambling requirements are satisfied?
16. How can a creator-payment scheme stay legally and economically separate from ordinary simulation currency?
17. What player claims arise if rewards are reduced, transfers frozen, listings abandoned or the game shuts down?
