# Hostel Hustle pilot content

This is the Phase 3 content handoff for the four-part Hostel Hustle episode. It is a content and implementation contract, not a claim that the episode is shipped. The Phase 3 brief asks for one location cluster, a recurring cast, two valid approaches with a later callback, guest and established-student entry, solo completion, late-return recap, and one exactly-once memento (`docs/research/allworld-retention-implementation-phases-2026-10-14.md:76-88`).

The cluster is the fictional UNILAG campus in Lagos. It already has a Main Gate, cafeteria/New Hall, library, engineering, Student Union, Senate, sports centre, auditorium and Lagoon Front (`src/campus/unilag/trail.ts:2-10`; `src/campus/unilag/content.ts:47-80`). The recurring resident is **Kola**, a Student Union volunteer at `student-union`; the supporting cast is Aunty Ngozi at the cafeteria, Tunde at Engineering, Bisi at the Library and Seun at the Auditorium (`src/campus/unilag/content.ts:80-88`). Dialogue below is authored, short, and human-focused. It uses Nigerian place language without presenting caricature as identity.

## Playable contract

The episode should use a durable `chapter`, `choice`, `outcomeTags`, `lastSeenAt`, and `mementoGranted` record keyed to the character, with the character ID as the owner of progress. Eligibility must be checked from saved outcomes and current rules; a button press or a model response alone must never complete a chapter. The new story record, chapter events, and memento entitlement are implementation work still required.

## Entry, assistance, and return paths

**Guest introduction.** A guest may enter through Main Gate, meet Kola, walk the trail, visit the Student Union and choose the authored help conversation. The guest gate explicitly keeps visiting, the trail, games and shuttle open while withholding enrolment, study, hostel, jobs and student actions (`src/game/systems/onboarding.ts:144-146`). When a guest chooses work or study, the episode should explain “Settle in before you enrol” and offer the help route or a later resume marker. It must not grant student status, a job, a hostel allocation, or course credit.

**Established student.** A matriculated or studying player can use the real campus rules: registered study actions, a current campus job, hostel allocation and sleep, clubs, discovery, quiz, penalties, and Union tables. Hostel allocation requires a registered term, the Senate spot, a valid hall, and the simulated fee (`src/campus/unilag/student.ts:449-470`). Hostel sleep requires the allocated current room and creates a timed `campus-study` action (`src/campus/unilag/student.ts:498-510`). Those are good optional context beats, but the episode must not make a hostel fee or room allocation the only way to finish.

**Solo and AI assistance.** The authored fallback is always available when a friend, table seat, live event, model, or agent is unavailable. An identified resident or approved AI character may deliver Kola's reaction, choose from the three authored prompts, or fill a supported table/help role. It cannot create a chapter completion, bypass student eligibility, mint the memento, or decide which outcome tag was earned. The current agent surface already labels the character and restricts actions; the episode needs a new story adapter that passes approved dialogue and provenance into the durable story record. No new eligibility rule should be inferred from a chat line.

**Late return.** On the next `get`/life settlement after `lastSeenAt`, show a compact recap rather than replaying completed chapters: “Kola kept the Union table open. You chose the study route; Bisi says the explanation is still helping. Games evening is ready.” The recap reads `chapter`, `choice`, and `outcomeTags`, then offers the next incomplete chapter. If the player returns after several days, expired event or table availability changes only the current activity option; it does not erase the saved choice or require the earlier prerequisite again.

## Phase 6 follow-on content proposals

**Second episode — “The Quiet Hours.”** Kola asks the player to help Bisi keep a late study corner usable after the games evening. Choices are repair a practical problem through a real home/furniture or campus activity, coach a classmate through a validated study step, or host a quiet welcome. The callback changes whether Bisi remembers the player as a fixer, teacher, or host. It ends with a cosmetic “library bookmark” memento, subject to the same exactly-once entitlement contract.

**Shared project variant — “Akoka Games Board.”** Several characters contribute exploration, a learnable table activity, or hosting/help. A future Phase 4/6 shared ledger should record role-specific contribution receipts and a stable occurrence ID; cash must not be the only useful contribution. Completion changes a visible Student Union board or event detail, while the Hostel Hustle memento remains character-owned. This is proposed content only; the current campus counter is not a shared project ledger (`docs/research/allworld-retention-implementation-phases-2026-10-14.md:94-98`).
