# Wardrobe fit implementation

Implemented in `src/scene/wardrobe/geometry.ts` and supported by the diagnostic controls in `scripts/body/wardrobe-preview.html`.

- Garment dimensions now derive from the female and male GLB rest-frame bounds. Short tops close over the pelvis shell; long robes use coherent left/right leg weights and omit covered upper-trouser geometry. The visible kaftan/agbada trouser ring shares the robe hem plane and four-bone weights, then converges to its own calf over 0.08 m.
- Sleeve roots blend from the chest into the upper arm and nest below the torso shoulder. The wrapper fold begins below the buba blouse. These changes remove shoulder spikes, waist wedges, underarm holes, and posed trouser intersections without another skeleton or draw call.
- The female short-sleeve cuff already had a 76 mm cloth radius around an arm measuring at most 55.3 mm from its centreline, so its close-up skin patch was a weight mismatch rather than an undersized envelope. At the cuff, shipped arm vertices remain about 84% upper-arm and 16% lower-arm, while the sleeve previously used its 84% geometric progress as 84% lower-arm. Short sleeves now follow the measured late joint transition and reach about 16.5% lower-arm weight at the cuff. Long and wide sleeve weights, the shoulder bridge, and all sleeve radii remain unchanged.
- The male `neck_01` bounds include the trapezius, so the first collar correction fitted the opening but left the garment rings below the actual shoulder surface. The accepted broad-shoulder path raises the shoulder ring by 85 mm and places the neck-base and neck-top rings 45 mm and 75 mm above the neck joint. It lowers only the front arc of the upper three rings with a smooth 45 mm squared-sine profile. Skinning still uses each ring's original raised height, preserving the chest-to-neck weight transition, while the cloth-front profile follows the lowered front. The female path keeps its established ring heights.
- Hair and accessories use measured head bounds. Braids use 12 varied five-point tapered strands, locs use nine thicker curved strands, and ponytail uses a fitted rear tie with five tapered strands. These hanging styles retain the shipped, runtime-tinted base hair as their anatomically fitted scalp and hairline; generated replacement caps and floating crown ridges were removed. Caps, head covers, glasses, and earrings continue to follow the fitted head envelope.
- The preview provides representative fit presets, expression and eye controls, a face camera, and an eight-second motion sample. Motion stops when hidden, respects reduced-motion, and leaves no independent timer or animation loop running.

## Final eyewear fit correction — 13 October 2026

A fresh side-view review found a remaining gap: whole-head max-Z was the nose tip, placing glasses about 33 mm ahead of the upper bridge. Measured face-UV anchors put the female eye plane at y=1.6540 and bridge z=0.0873, male y=1.6970 and z=0.0901. The renderer now uses rest-frame-relative eye centers and bridge+6 mm lens planes. Four-sided temple tubes join the actual rim edges and the fitted head side; the bridge overlaps the inner rims. Glasses and sunglasses retain the head weights, existing draw and skeleton and each lose eight triangles (156 and 52 respectively).

The parent verified finite geometry and valid indices for both bodies and both items, and browser-accepted female front/side/walking views plus male seated glasses and walking sunglasses. The final body/avatar suite passed 42/42, followed by full typecheck, production build and measured download budgets. No other wardrobe path changed in this correction.
