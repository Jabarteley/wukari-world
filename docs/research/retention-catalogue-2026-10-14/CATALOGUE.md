**Allworld comparative game catalogue: 134 core award winners and seven additional recognition cases**

Research date: 14 October 2026. This is an extensive, purposive design sample, not a ranking of the most addictive games or a representative survey of the entire industry. Awards and documented mechanics are evidence; explanations of return motives and Allworld transfers are analysis. No cross-game retention dataset establishes which mechanic caused commercial success.

**What is counted**

The core contains 134 distinct titles with a verified named game award. A further seven records are separated below: four pre-release showcase awards, two prototype-era awards, and one platform category honoree. Thus there are 141 verified recognition records, without treating all recognitions as equivalent. Three initially proposed award claims failed verification and are excluded. The 12 historical comparator cards and the focused Whiteout Survival/Candy Crush research do not inflate the core count.

An award in sound, narrative, multiplayer, platform design, or another category is not labelled an overall Game of the Year win. These records establish recognition of the named title or edition, not its player retention. The cited award years run from 2000 to 2025; these are not release years. Historical cards separately record original release years.

Live-game features change. Mechanics are described in the period or edition supported by their sources; this catalogue does not promise that an old event, service, or progression rule is currently available. Original Overwatch, CS:GO, Destiny, Halo 3, Forza Horizon 4, and modern re-releases need their stated edition notes. A finite story can make someone return to finish it without creating an indefinitely recurring habit.

**How human desires are mapped**

Every card has two to five analyst-selected motives: mastery/competence, autonomy/agency, belonging/care, esteem/recognition, curiosity/discovery, identity/expression, ownership/stewardship, purpose/contribution, or comfort/continuity. The first three overlap with established SDT constructs; the remaining labels are design lenses, not additional proven universal basic needs. A tag identifies a plausible connection to a documented mechanic. It does not measure how much that motive explains a game’s retention.

**Detailed core cards**

**1. Clash of Clans**

mobile strategy · Return pattern: daily cadence plus social progression.

**Verified recognition:** Google Play Best Multi-device Game, 2024 — Best multi-device game (US). [Award source](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/google-play/google-play-best-apps-games-2024/) Google Play's 2024 winner page names Clash of Clans as Best multi-device game in the US.

**Human desires, inferred:** Mastery / competence; Belonging / care; Ownership / stewardship; Esteem / recognition.

**Documented mechanics:**

- **Clan Games tiers:** Individual challenges add to shared clan points; tiers unlock for the whole clan and maxing personal points adds a reward choice. [Mechanic source](https://support.supercell.com/clash-of-clans/en/articles/clan-games-rewards.html)
- **Daily Star Bonus:** Five stars complete a bonus, then a 24-hour timer starts; up to two bonuses can stack and higher leagues pay more. [Mechanic source](https://support.supercell.com/clash-of-clans/en/articles/about-multiplayer-and-trophies-2.html)

**Why another session may appeal:** A short daily target feeds a social, longer-horizon clan ladder, with visible progress and a reason to return before the timer expires.

**Allworld transfer:** Pair one daily activity threshold with a neighbourhood meter and a visible shared reward tier.

**Transfer risk:** Timers and clan pressure can make low-value actions feel compulsory.

**2. Pokémon GO**

location-based collection RPG · Return pattern: habitual exploration plus collection.

**Verified recognition:** BAFTA Games Award, 2017 — Mobile Game. [Award source](https://www.bafta.org/awards/games/mobile-game/) BAFTA's Mobile Game archive marks Pokémon GO as the 2017 winner.

**Human desires, inferred:** Curiosity / discovery; Ownership / stewardship; Belonging / care.

**Documented mechanics:**

- **Location discovery and collection:** Niantic describes exploring the real world to find Pokémon, with PokéStops, Gyms and a growing Pokédex. [Mechanic source](https://pokemongolive.com/en/post/pokemongofest2024/)
- **Daily streak bonuses:** The official support documentation describes daily catch and PokéStop streaks that build across consecutive days for increasing rewards. [Mechanic source](https://niantic.helpshift.com/hc/en/6-pokemon-go/faq/2507-what-are-daily-bonuses/)

**Why another session may appeal:** Place-based novelty and collection combine with a consecutive-day bonus that makes the next outing legible.

**Allworld transfer:** Tie discovery of real or simulated places to a collection book and forgiving daily activity streak.

**Transfer risk:** Location mechanics can exclude players without safe, accessible routes or nearby content.

**3. Monument Valley**

perspective puzzle adventure · Return pattern: episodic discovery.

**Verified recognition:** Apple Design Award, 2014 — Game design winner. [Award source](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/monument-valley/id728293409) Apple's App Store listing explicitly identifies Monument Valley as a 2014 Apple Design Award winner.

**Human desires, inferred:** Mastery / competence; Curiosity / discovery.

**Documented mechanics:**

- **Hand-authored puzzle chapters:** The official listing describes guiding Ida through discrete monuments, uncovering hidden paths and manipulating impossible geometry. [Mechanic source](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/monument-valley/id728293409)
- **Optional chapter expansion:** The listing offers additional chapters as a separate purchase, extending the authored journey without changing the core puzzle language. [Mechanic source](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/monument-valley/id728293409)

**Why another session may appeal:** A new chapter is a self-contained invitation to return, with discovery and completion carrying the motivation instead of a daily timer.

**Allworld transfer:** Build short, authored resident or landmark episodes with a clear reveal and optional adjacent stories.

**Transfer risk:** Purely authored content is expensive to produce and can run out without a durable social loop.

**4. Monument Valley 2**

perspective puzzle adventure · Return pattern: story-led episodic discovery.

**Verified recognition:** The Game Awards, 2017 — Best Mobile Game. [Award source](https://thegameawards.com/rewind/year-2017) The Game Awards' 2017 edition archive names Monument Valley 2 as Best Mobile Game.

**Human desires, inferred:** Mastery / competence; Curiosity / discovery.

**Documented mechanics:**

- **Two-character puzzle relationship:** ustwo describes guiding a mother and child through changing architecture, with new interactions built around their dynamics. [Mechanic source](https://ustwogames.co.uk/our-games/monument-valley-2/)
- **Transforming levels:** Levels change through light, pathways and geometry, creating fresh state changes rather than repeated grind. [Mechanic source](https://ustwogames.co.uk/our-games/monument-valley-2/)

**Why another session may appeal:** Narrative relationship and novel puzzle states give each return a specific emotional or mechanical question to resolve.

**Allworld transfer:** Use paired character arcs where a player’s action changes the relationship and opens the next place or scene.

**Transfer risk:** Novelty-heavy puzzles can frustrate players if the interaction grammar is not taught clearly.

**5. Alto's Odyssey**

endless runner · Return pattern: mode-flexible replay.

**Verified recognition:** Apple Design Award, 2018 — Apple Design Award winner. [Award source](https://www.builtbysnowman.com/press/) Snowman's official press page records Alto's Odyssey as a 2018 Apple Design Award winner.

**Human desires, inferred:** Mastery / competence; Comfort / continuity.

**Documented mechanics:**

- **Biome-based runs:** The official game page describes an endless sandboarding journey through evolving landscapes with new movement opportunities. [Mechanic source](https://altosodyssey.com/)
- **Zen Mode:** The developer documents a score-free mode focused on movement and scenery, giving the same system a lower-pressure return path. [Mechanic source](https://altosodyssey.com/)

**Why another session may appeal:** The same route system supports mastery, exploration and calm play, allowing different return motives without separate products.

**Allworld transfer:** Give Allworld activities both a challenge track and a relaxed exploration track with shared cosmetics.

**Transfer risk:** Multiple modes can split attention and make the core reward economy harder to explain.

**6. Florence**

interactive narrative · Return pattern: episodic emotional progression.

**Verified recognition:** BAFTA Games Award, 2019 — Mobile Game. [Award source](https://www.bafta.org/awards/games/mobile-game/) BAFTA's Mobile Game archive marks Florence as the 2019 winner.

**Human desires, inferred:** Belonging / care; Curiosity / discovery.

**Documented mechanics:**

- **Interactive vignette chapters:** The official listing describes a wordless story told through short interactive scenes and everyday interactions. [Mechanic source](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/florence/id1297430468)
- **Relationship progression:** The scenes change as Florence’s relationship develops, using interaction difficulty and rhythm to express emotional state. [Mechanic source](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/florence/id1297430468)

**Why another session may appeal:** Players return to complete the next emotional beat, with changing interaction language making the narrative itself the progression ladder.

**Allworld transfer:** Use short resident vignettes where task form changes with trust, conflict or intimacy rather than only increasing numbers.

**Transfer risk:** A highly authored emotional arc may not support indefinite daily replay.

**7. Threes!**

number puzzle · Return pattern: personal mastery.

**Verified recognition:** Apple Design Award, 2014 — Game design winner. [Award source](https://asherv.com/threes/presskit/) The developer press kit lists the 2014 Apple Design Award as a win for Threes!.

**Human desires, inferred:** Mastery / competence; Autonomy / agency.

**Documented mechanics:**

- **Finite board with emergent combinations:** The developer describes a tiny puzzle whose rules create a deep challenge that can be played repeatedly. [Mechanic source](https://asherv.com/threes/presskit/)
- **Score and best-run pursuit:** Each run produces a score and tile history, making improvement and personal records the persistent reward. [Mechanic source](https://asherv.com/threes/presskit/)

**Why another session may appeal:** A complete run is short enough to retry immediately, and personal bests turn failure into information rather than a dead end.

**Allworld transfer:** Offer compact repeatable civic puzzles or table challenges with personal records and no mandatory timer.

**Transfer risk:** A self-score loop can lack social belonging or a narrative reason to return.

**8. Honkai: Star Rail**

turn-based RPG · Return pattern: live-service progression.

**Verified recognition:** Google Play Best Game, 2023 — Best game (US). [Award source](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/google-play/google-play-best-apps-games-2023/) Google Play's 2023 US winner page names Honkai: Star Rail as Best Game.

**Human desires, inferred:** Curiosity / discovery; Identity / expression; Ownership / stewardship.

**Documented mechanics:**

- **Quest and character progression:** The official listing describes story exploration, turn-based combat, character building and a growing roster. [Mechanic source](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.HoYoverse.hkrpgoversea)
- **Live events and resource cycles:** HoYoverse describes continual updates and events that add stories, characters and limited activities. [Mechanic source](https://www.hoyolab.com/article/19256380)

**Why another session may appeal:** Persistent roster investment makes each session useful, while new authored content supplies periodic reasons to come back.

**Allworld transfer:** Separate durable resident relationships from rotating city events, so returning players retain identity while finding novelty.

**Transfer risk:** Live-service calendars and random acquisition can create fatigue and spending pressure.

**9. Genshin Impact**

open-world action RPG · Return pattern: open-world habit plus exploration.

**Verified recognition:** Apple App Store iPhone Game of the Year, 2020 — iPhone game of the year. [Award source](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2020/12/apple-presents-app-store-best-of-2020-winners/) Apple's Best of 2020 winner release names Genshin Impact as iPhone Game of the Year.

**Human desires, inferred:** Curiosity / discovery; Autonomy / agency; Identity / expression; Ownership / stewardship.

**Documented mechanics:**

- **Open-world exploration:** HoYoverse describes a large world with regions, puzzles, quests, collectibles and elemental traversal. [Mechanic source](https://genshin.hoyoverse.com/en/)
- **Original Resin cadence:** HoYoWiki documents Original Resin as a resource that regenerates over time and is spent to claim rewards from repeatable activities. [Mechanic source](https://wiki.hoyolab.com/pc/genshin/entry/2970?lang=en-us)

**Why another session may appeal:** A persistent world rewards both planned daily maintenance and unstructured discovery, covering short and long sessions.

**Allworld transfer:** Combine daily resident errands with open-ended street discovery and a bounded resource that prevents endless grinding.

**Transfer risk:** Energy gates and random character acquisition can feel extractive or make content completion costly.

**Transfer risk:** Creation tools require moderation, discoverability, and a clear quality bar; empty social spaces can expose weak content supply.
