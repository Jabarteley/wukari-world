## What transfers to Allworld

The set points to four compatible return structures: a small daily action, a short repeatable mastery loop, a longer authored episode, and a social or seasonal layer. Allworld can combine these through daily venue or resident tasks, short table or street challenges, resident arcs and landmark mysteries, neighbourhood goals, and seasonal cosmetics. Catch-up, optional exploration, and non-power rewards reduce pressure from missed days.

The main design risk is turning a living world into a checklist. Use missions to reveal places, people, and relationships already present in Allworld. Measure completion, abandonment, next-session return, social participation, recovery use, and reward redemption before treating any mechanic as a retention improvement.
