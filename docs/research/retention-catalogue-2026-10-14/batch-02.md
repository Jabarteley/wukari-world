# Retention Catalogue Batch 02 — competitive and live games

Research date: 2026-10-14. Award claims use organizer or primary-holder records and distinguish wins from nominations. Mechanics are documented features; return reasons and Allworld transfers are design inferences, not causal retention claims. “Ongoing” describes a live or persistent loop; it does not assert current commercial health.

## Fortnite

BAFTA Games Award, 2019, Evolving Game ([winner record](https://www.bafta.org/media-centre/press-releases/winners-announced-for-the-british-academy-games-awards-in-2019/)). Epic’s Battle Pass spans pages of rewards and earns XP across Battle Royale, Creative, LEGO Fortnite and Festival ([support](https://www.epicgames.com/help/c-32735058/c-EpicGamesStore_About/a21682761?lang=en-US)). Daily, weekly and character quests provide repeatable targets, while the island and creator experiences keep changing. The documented loop is cross-mode progression plus seasonal content. Inference: humans return for a visible unfinished pass, a changed social space and shared events. Allworld can transfer this into seasonal city festivals and cross-job civic quests, with badges for contribution rather than combat power. Risk: FOMO and cadence pressure; provide catch-up and permanent core access. Retention: ongoing live service.

## Apex Legends

BAFTA Games Award, 2020, Multiplayer ([winner record](https://www.bafta.org/media-centre/press-releases/winners-announced-british-academy-games-awards-2020/)). EA documents roughly three-month seasons with new Legends, loot and events; Battle Passes award cosmetics, currency and packs through play ([FAQ](https://www.ea.com/games/apex-legends/about/frequently-asked-questions)). Daily and weekly challenges award Stars, and a free track remains available. Ranked Leagues and the ALGS add a formal skill path ([rules](https://media.contentapi.ea.com/content/dam/apex-legends/compete/official-rules-updated/algs-year-4/en-algs-year-4-official-rules-2023-08-21.pdf)). Inference: squads return for a combination of coordination, mastery and the next cosmetic milestone. Allworld can use role-based city teams, fair matchmaking and seasonal civic leagues. Risk: split cadence and rank anxiety; keep essential progression forgiving. Retention: ongoing live service.

## VALORANT

Esports Awards, 2022, Esports Game of the Year ([winner release](https://esportsawards.com/2022-winners/)). Riot’s Battle Pass article documents Episodes, Acts, a new pass per Act, free and premium tracks, and simultaneous progress from missions and matches ([official guide](https://playvalorant.com/en-gb/news/game-updates/what-is-the-valorant-battlepass/)). Official patch notes document Ranked Rating and matchmaking adjustments ([patch notes](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/game-updates/valorant-patch-notes-13-00/)). The documented return hooks are short seasonal windows, missions, contracts and visible competitive rating. Inference: people come back to practise, maintain team routines and complete an expiring Act. Allworld can apply transparent ratings to jobs and team missions, with badges for helpful human contribution. Risk: rank pressure and premium status; support unranked social play. Retention: ongoing competitive service.

## Overwatch

BAFTA Games Award, 2017, Multiplayer ([winner release](https://www.bafta.org/media-centre/press-releases/winners-announced-for-the-british-academy-games-awards-in-2017/)). This record refers to the original 2016 Overwatch. Blizzard's October 2017 original-game page documents Competitive Play placement matches, skill rating and named tiers ([historical season page](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/21173548/)). Overwatch 2-era Battle Pass, title and live-season sources were removed because they do not describe the award-winning edition. Historical inference: visible placements and tiers supported mastery, rank recovery and team coordination. Allworld can borrow transparent skill tiers and complementary roles without transferring sequel systems backward. Retention: historical competitive service for original Overwatch.

## League of Legends

The Game Awards, 2019, Best Esports Game ([official rewind](https://thegameawards.com/rewind/year-2019)). Riot’s ranked notes document season resets, provisional games and ranked reward missions ([2026 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-26-1-notes/)); other official notes add seasonal challenges alongside ranked splits ([14.1 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-14-1-notes/)). Riot’s ranked archive shows continuing updates and reward changes ([ranked hub](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/tags/ranked/)). The documented loop is a large champion roster plus repeated split goals and patches. Inference: players return to learn a changed meta, climb and play with established teams. Allworld can use seasonal job ladders and durable civic résumés. Risk: complexity and toxicity; add AI coaching and moderation. Retention: ongoing competitive service.

## Dota 2

Valve’s official Steam listing records an IGN People’s Choice Award at gamescom 2011 ([official store listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/570/Dota_2/)); this is the batch’s weaker award-source case because the organizer archive was not available in the retrieval window. Valve’s matchmaking update documents Rank and Rank Confidence, Glicko calibration and improved treatment of returning players ([New Frontiers](https://www.dota2.com/newfrontiers)). The official store listing also documents multiplayer, co-op and replay support. Inference: the large hero pool and calibration let people return to test new drafts and improve without a permanent placement penalty. Allworld can use confidence-based job matchmaking and reversible role experimentation. Risk: onboarding and toxicity; evidence confidence is medium. Retention: ongoing competitive service.

## Counter-Strike: Global Offensive

Esports Awards, 2016, Esports Game of the Year ([winner archive](https://esportsawards.com/previous-winners/esports-awards-2016/)). This record refers to historical CS:GO; Steam app 730 now resolves to Counter-Strike 2 and is not used as evidence. Valve's archived [CS:GO overview](https://blog.counter-strike.net/about/) documents objective-based rounds, the in-game economy and Competitive Skill Groups. The official [Operation Shattered Web page](https://www.counter-strike.net/shatteredweb) documents weekly mission cards, Stars, an upgradeable coin, weapon collections, agents and stickers. Historical inference: compact rematches, visible skill and operation goals reinforced a mastery loop. Allworld can borrow short team tasks and non-power identity rewards while keeping CS:GO distinct from its replacement. Retention: historical competitive live service.

## Rocket League

BAFTA Games Award, 2017, Evolving Game ([winner release](https://www.bafta.org/media-centre/press-releases/winners-announced-for-the-british-academy-games-awards-in-2017/)). Psyonix documents placement matches, ranks, seasonal rewards and titles ([rewards](https://www.rocketleague.com/news/introducing-rocket-league-season-5-rewards)). Competitive seasons, tournaments and Rocket Pass share a schedule ([season overview](https://www.rocketleague.com/news/rocket-league-free-to-play--seasons--new-ranks--and-more)), while weekly, season and event challenges award XP, items and Drops ([challenge guide](https://www.rocketleague.com/news/rocket-league-challenges--a-closer-look)). Inference: players return for mechanical practice, team chemistry and a clean seasonal target. Allworld can use short city skill trials and cooperative missions with titles and home décor. Risk: resets and gates frustrate late entrants; preserve fair placement and catch-up. Retention: ongoing competitive service.

## Splatoon

The Game Awards, 2015, Best Multiplayer Game ([official rewind](https://thegameawards.com/rewind/year-2015)); Japan Game Awards, 2016, Grand Award ([organizer record](https://awards.cesa.or.jp/2016/en/prize/year/01.html)). Nintendo documents Turf War’s simple territory objective, gear that changes abilities and the expressive ink movement. Nintendo’s Splatfest archive documents limited-time themed team events ([Splatfest history](https://splatoon.nintendo.com/base/en/gallery/splatfests/)). The documented combination is approachable rules plus mastery, collection and event identity. Inference: humans return for quick social matches, team belonging and a playstyle they can display. Allworld can offer approachable neighborhood competitions, seasonal votes and cosmetic civic identity. Risk: event timing and team imbalance; archive achievements and provide practice. Retention: ongoing competitive service.

## Splatoon 3

Japan Game Awards, 2023, Award for Excellence ([organizer record](https://awards.cesa.or.jp/2023/en/prize/year/05.html)). Nintendo documents three-team Splatfests, Tricolor Turf War, seasonal catalogs, Anarchy objective modes and four-player Salmon Run co-op ([official Direct recap](https://splatoon.nintendo.com/en/news/catch-up-on-all-the-latest-from-the-splatoon-3-direct/)). Catalog points exchange for seasonal gear, ability chunks, titles, banners and decorations. The documented loop supports competition, cooperation and collection without changing the basic ink rule. Inference: players return because they can switch motivations while keeping one identity. Allworld can combine civic elections, disaster-response teams, job ladders and seasonal catalogs. Risk: too many currencies; make the primary social loop clear. Retention: ongoing competitive service.

## Destiny

D.I.C.E. Awards, 2015, Outstanding Achievement in Online Gameplay ([AIAS winners](https://www.interactive.org/news/18th_dice_awards_winners_.asp)). This is Destiny 1, which Bungie now labels a legacy product that will not receive planned game updates or content. The [legacy activity guide](https://help.bungie.net/hc/en-us/articles/360049201951-Legacy-Support-for-Destiny-1-Activities) documents remaining weekly resets, Nightfall and playlist rewards, raid checkpoints, hard modes, challenges, strikes and Crucible playlists. Historical inference: those systems created coordinated team rituals and a weekly goal calendar. Allworld can study cooperative checkpoints and durable badges as a legacy pattern, without presenting Destiny 1 as a current ongoing-service recommendation. Retention: historical/legacy live service.

## Destiny 2

The Game Awards, 2019, Best Community Support ([official rewind](https://thegameawards.com/rewind/year-2019)). Bungie documents seasonal challenges released week by week, XP and rewards, and completion before season end ([challenge announcement](https://www.bungie.net/7/en/News/article/50040)). Guardian Ranks display knowledge and skill, with elite ranks requiring commendations from fellow players ([new-player guide](https://www.bungie.net/7/en/News/Article/official-destiny-2-guide-new)). Weekly resets cover raids, Nightfalls, Trials, bounties and powerful rewards ([reset guide](https://help.bungie.net/hc/en-us/articles/360049199911-Destiny-2-Ritual-Reset-Guide)). Inference: players return for fireteam routines, title paths and build improvement. Allworld can use human commendations and explainable AI. Risk: Bungie says planned live-service updates ended in 2026; transfer durable progression without copying churn. Retention: ongoing/legacy transition.

## Final Fantasy XIV

BAFTA Games Award, 2023, Evolving Game ([category record](https://www.bafta.org/awards/games/evolving-game/)). The official manual documents a Challenge Log with rewards and weekly reset, plus achievements and duty lists ([manual](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/game_manual/view/)). Patch notes document Duty Roulettes, daily challenges, tomestone rewards and duties counting toward achievements ([patch 5.4](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/lodestone/topics/detail/230f6e0af3536f9a40654d783c8c2f1094131070)). The Lodestone database exposes thousands of achievements with title and equipment rewards ([database](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/lodestone/playguide/db/achievement/)). Inference: players return for social routines, story continuation and a character identity that accumulates. Allworld can make jobs, homes and city achievements coexist with weekly cooperative duties. Risk: breadth and time demand; support catch-up and asynchronous contribution. Retention: ongoing MMO.

## World of Warcraft

AIAS/D.I.C.E. Award, 2005, Massively Multiplayer/Persistent World Game of the Year ([winner record](https://www.interactive.org/awards/2005_8th_awards.asp)). AIAS describes Azeroth as a persistent online RPG built around exploration, quests and challenges ([game record](https://www.interactive.org/games/video_game_details.asp?idAward=2005&idGame=105)). Current Blizzard content continues the pattern through raids, Mythic+ and rotating seasons ([news hub](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news)). The documented foundation is a persistent character and world; the return inference is guild reconnection, story continuation and prestige pursuit. Allworld can make cities persistent places where residents build work histories, homes, relationships and public badges, with optional crews. Risk: legacy complexity and MMO time demand; support episodic participation and clear AI onboarding. Retention: ongoing MMO.

## Sea of Thieves

BAFTA Games Award, 2021, Evolving Game ([category record](https://www.bafta.org/awards/games/evolving-game/)). Rare documents Guilds of up to 24 pirates, offline pledged ships and shared Guild Reputation ([Season Ten preview](https://www.seaofthieves.com/news/season-ten-preview)). Season 11 added direct Voyage launch, on-demand Raid Voyages and reputation on completion ([launch article](https://www.seaofthieves.com/news/season11-launch)). Captaincy Milestones track long-term playstyle and award customization, alongside seasonal commendations ([Milestones update](https://www.seaofthieves.com/news/milestones-update)). Inference: crews return for shared stories, status and emergent surprise. Allworld should make human crews, neighborhoods and guilds central, with fair AI filling gaps rather than replacing agency. Risk: emergent PvP loss can feel hostile; preserve safe social spaces. Retention: ongoing social sandbox.

## Tom Clancy’s Rainbow Six Siege

Game Critics Awards: Best of E3 2014, Best PC Game — pre-release showcase ([organizer winners](https://www.gamecriticsawards.com/2014winners.html)). Ubisoft’s Ranked 3.0 update documents placement matches, rank progression and a Competitive Rewards track ([update](https://www.ubisoft.com/en-us/game/rainbow-six/siege/news-updates/5fzYRZKVVHqqRkkv3m4MyF)). The commendation system lets teammates award Valor, Dedication or Guidance; consistent commendations produce a visible streak and pack bonus ([system guide](https://www.ubisoft.com/en-ca/game/rainbow-six/siege/news-updates/2YtRrSCEi2eWoYEksA44FV/commendation-system-how-it-works)). Ubisoft also documents rank, reputation and challenge badges on Operator cards ([progression guide](https://news.ubisoft.com/en-us/article/4luBBDMfipI6BDrwGGMV0L/rainbow-six-siege--operation-collision-point-operator-remaster-and-balancing-guide)). Inference: players return to refine knowledge, preserve squad trust and prove improvement. Allworld can use explainable reputation, human commendations and fair AI. Risk: toxicity and smurfing; include appeals and practice spaces. Retention: ongoing competitive service.

## Audit notes

All 16 supplied titles have verified exact recognition. Fifteen are post-release award wins; Rainbow Six Siege is separately labelled as a Best of E3 2014 pre-release showcase winner and must not be counted as an equivalent post-release major award. Dota 2 remains the weakest source case because the accessible primary record is Valve’s official Steam page and its embedded `ign-pca-gamescom2011` award asset rather than an organizer archive. Sea of Thieves is correctly the BAFTA 2021 Evolving Game winner, and Final Fantasy XIV Online is correctly the 2023 winner. Return reasons are deliberately phrased as inferences from documented systems; no causal retention claim or player metric is asserted.
