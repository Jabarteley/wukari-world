# Retention catalogue batch 03

This batch contains 16 supplied strategy and life-sim titles. Award claims use organizer or first-party developer evidence, and special recognition is labelled at its actual scope. Mechanics are grounded in official game, support, manual, or developer pages. “Return reason” and “Allworld transfer” are design analysis, not measured retention claims.

Fifteen titles have exact verified recognition: Animal Crossing: New Horizons; Minecraft; The Sims; The Sims 2; Sid Meier's Civilization IV; Sid Meier's Civilization V; Sid Meier's Civilization VI; Age of Empires IV; StarCraft II: Wings of Liberty; Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos; Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle; Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope; Fire Emblem: Three Houses; XCOM: Enemy Unknown; and Into the Breach.

Stardew Valley is deferred. PlayStation.Blog's 2016 reader-poll page gives it a Bronze placement in Best Independent Game; Firewatch received the Platinum first-place award. Bronze is recorded accurately in the JSON but is not counted as an award win.

Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle's Best Strategy Game recognition came from the Game Critics Awards' Best of E3 2017 pre-release showcase. It is verified but explicitly separated from post-release major awards. Animal Crossing: New Horizons did win BAFTA's 2021 Game Beyond Entertainment category and also won Multiplayer. The Sims did win AIAS's 2000 Game of the Year. The Sims and The Sims 2 mechanics use EA's modern Legacy Collection re-release pages only where those pages explicitly describe the original games' carried-forward core designs; the JSON distinguishes the award-winning editions from the modern releases.

Reserve gaps: XCOM 2, Pikmin 4, Cities: Skylines, and Frostpunk remain unverified reserves. Evidence count: 15 verified entries, including one pre-release showcase winner; 1 deferred supplied title; 4 reserve gaps.
