# Batch 04 — mastery, puzzles and roguelikes

This batch treats return as a designed consequence of agency, learning and meaning. Hades, Dead Cells, Returnal, FTL and Vampire Survivors use runs: changing layouts or builds make replay legible, while persistence turns failure into progress. Balatro compresses that loop into combinatorial experiments. Inscryption and Outer Wilds make curiosity itself the progression; Obra Dinn, Baba Is You, Cocoon, Portal, Portal 2 and Braid turn a new mental model into the reward. Papers, Please makes replay ethical and economic, while Inside uses authored mystery and escalation. For Allworld, the transfer is a social life game: real cities, homes, careers, human and AI agents, missions and achievements should remember choices, expose understandable systems and offer multiple meaningful routes. These are campaign return and replay patterns, not claims about daily live-service retention.

## Hades

BAFTA’s 2021 Best Game winner, Hades makes a failed escape attempt productive: chambers, foes, rewards and encounters remix, while House relationships, weapons and upgrades persist. Its return loop is unusually strong because narrative and mastery advance together. Allworld can borrow the structure for city missions: vary conditions and contacts, preserve home, career and reputation state, and let agents remember consequences. The risk is turning meta-progression into chores or making the optimal build crowd out social experimentation. This is replay plus campaign return, not daily-live retention. Sources: [BAFTA 2021 winners release](https://www.bafta.org/media-centre/press-releases/winners-announced-british-academy-games-awards-2021/), [Supergiant](https://www.supergiantgames.com/games/hades/), [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1145360/Hades/).

## Balatro

The 2025 Game Developers Choice Game of the Year, Balatro turns poker hands into a run-based laboratory. Players score blinds, buy cards and stack Joker effects; the interesting return decision is whether to repeat a reliable line or chase a new interaction. For Allworld, the useful transfer is combinatorial missions: skills, contacts, tools and local knowledge should interact in surprising but learnable ways, unlocking achievements when players discover a route. Luck and opaque synergies are risks, so the system needs readable feedback and room for social meaning. This is replay mastery. Sources: [GDCA](https://gdconf.com/article/-balatro-plays-winning-hand-at-gdca-2025-receiving-game-of-the-year/), [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2379780/Balatro/).

## Dead Cells

The Game Awards’ 2018 Best Action Game, Dead Cells combines procedural biomes, precise combat and permanent unlocks. A run teaches enemy patterns and movement while blueprints and cells expand future options. The Allworld transfer is a city route layer with changing districts, escalating mission modifiers and career or tool unlocks that widen agency without erasing execution skill. Accessibility matters: procedural variety should change decisions, not merely scenery, and optimal routes should not dominate every run. This is replay mastery. Sources: [The Game Awards official winner video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jzMQ8l7-MMM), [Motion Twin/Steam product page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/588650/Dead_Cells/).

## Returnal

BAFTA’s 2022 Best Game winner, Returnal uses a hostile time loop, bullet-hell movement and persistent discoveries. Death resets most of the run, but some equipment, traversal abilities and narrative discoveries persist across cycles. Allworld can use recurring city crises with changed variables: preserve discovered facts, trusted contacts and access routes, then ask players to execute better rather than simply grind levels. The risk is excessive punishment and long sessions; the persistent layer must make failure feel informative. This is replay plus mystery campaign return. Sources: [BAFTA 2022 winners release](https://www.bafta.org/media-centre/press-releases/bafta-games-awards-2022-winners-announced/), [PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/returnal/), [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1649240/Returnal/).

## Vampire Survivors

BAFTA’s 2023 Best Game winner, Vampire Survivors makes the player steer through waves while weapons fire automatically. Timed survival, offered upgrades, weapon evolutions and explicit unlocks create a low-input power curve. Allworld can transfer the clarity rather than the combat: accessible missions where positioning and prioritization matter, with transparent achievement chains opening careers, neighborhoods or tools. The risk is passive play and solved builds; agents and social choices should keep agency visible. This is replay and achievement completion, not evidence of daily retention. Sources: [BAFTA 2023 winners release](https://www.bafta.org/media-centre/press-releases/bafta-announces-the-winners-of-the-2023-bafta-games-awards/), [poncle](https://poncle.games/vampire-survivors), [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1794680/Vampire_Survivors/).

## FTL: Faster Than Light

The 2013 IGF Excellence in Design winner, FTL turns a sector map into resource triage. Players power ship systems, assign crew, choose branching routes and pause real-time battles; permadeath makes every run a story of constrained judgment. Allworld can model work, health, home and relationships as competing systems across real city routes, with understandable tradeoffs and consequences. Avoid arbitrary events and scarcity that prevents experimentation. The return pattern is replay mastery: another run offers another route and a better-informed decision policy. Sources: [IGF organizer's 2013 winners release](https://ubmchannelnews.mediaroom.com/index.php?item=136985&s=29573), [Subset Games](https://subsetgames.com/ftl.html), [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/212680/FTL_Faster_Than_Light/).

## Inscryption

BAFTA’s 2022 Game Design winner, Inscryption combines sacrifice-based deckbuilding, branching encounters and meta-level shifts that change how the player understands the game. Its return impulse is curiosity plus mastery: players come back to test hidden rules and reinterpret earlier scenes. Allworld can borrow optional investigations in which agents, artifacts and schedules reveal deeper civic systems over time, while keeping the everyday social layer coherent. The main risks are spoiler sensitivity and a post-twist loss of replay value. This is mystery campaign return with selective replay. Sources: [BAFTA Game Design](https://www.bafta.org/awards/games/game-design/), [Devolver](https://www.devolverdigital.com/games/inscryption), [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1092790/Inscryption/).

## Outer Wilds

BAFTA’s 2020 Best Game winner, Outer Wilds uses a 22-minute solar-system loop in which knowledge is the only true progression. Players pilot freely, collect clues and update a ship log; understanding changes what is possible in the next loop. For Allworld, build city mysteries whose evidence persists in a civic knowledge map and unlocks routes, missions and agent conversations. The risk is finite replay after the mystery is solved, so the pattern suits authored campaign return rather than ongoing daily retention. Sources: [BAFTA winners release](https://static.bafta.org/uploads_pre_202411/1baftagames2020winnersrelease.pdf), [Mobius](https://www.mobiusdigitalgames.com/outer-wilds.html), [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/753640/Outer_Wilds/).

## Return of the Obra Dinn

BAFTA’s 2019 Game Design winner, Return of the Obra Dinn is a deduction system: inspect deaths with a pocket watch, connect names to uniforms and relationships, and confirm identities across scenes. The return reason is hypothesis testing, not grinding. Allworld can use resident cases built from schedules, social clues and artifacts in a shared evidence graph, with multiple valid investigation orders and achievements for well-supported conclusions. The risk is demanding note-taking and frustration when clues are weak. This is campaign mastery. Sources: [BAFTA Game Design](https://www.bafta.org/awards/games/game-design/), [Lucas Pope](https://3909llc.com/obra-dinn/), [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/653530/Return_of_the_Obra_Dinn/).

## Papers, Please

The 2014 Game Developers Choice Innovation Award winner, Papers, Please turns document inspection into a daily pressure puzzle. Players compare permits and passports, process people for wages and decide whether to obey rules when family survival or human appeals intervene. For Allworld, careers can present accountable decisions with visible rules, personal costs and alternate policies; replay should expose different endings and ethical routes. The risk is clerical repetition and opaque punishment. This is branching campaign replay, with meaningful return driven by choices rather than a live-service calendar. Sources: [GDCA](https://gdconf.com/article/the-last-of-us-wins-top-honors-at-game-developers-choice-awards/), [official site](https://papersplea.se/), [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/239030/Papers_Please/).

## Baba Is You

Baba Is You won IGF 2018 Excellence in Design for a system in which movable word blocks define the rules of the level. Players can make Baba the player, Flag the win condition or alter what an object is; every solution is a conceptual reframe. Allworld can expose selected city systems as manipulable, understandable rules so that players experiment with roles, permissions and relationships rather than merely select menus. The risk is sharp difficulty spikes and the mismatch between literal puzzle rules and messy social life. This is mastery puzzle progression. Sources: [developer's explicit IGF win record](https://www.hempuli.com/games/games.php?title=baba), [Hempuli](https://hempuli.com/baba/), [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/736260/Baba_Is_You/).

## Cocoon

The Game Awards’ 2023 Best Debut Indie Game, Cocoon nests worlds inside orbs. Players carry an orb, enter it, use its power and return to the larger world; environmental puzzles and guardian encounters teach each layer’s logic. The Allworld transfer is structural: city, home and career contexts can be nested, with relationships or skills acquired in one sphere unlocking actions in another. The risk is that a tightly authored puzzle arc has limited post-completion replay. This is campaign mastery. Sources: [Xbox’s official winners recap](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2023/12/07/xbox-game-awards-2023/), [Geometric Interactive](https://geometricinteractive.com/cocoon), [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1497440/COCOON/).

## Portal

Portal won the 2008 Game Developers Choice Game of the Year. Its portal gun, physics and compact test chambers teach one rule at a time before combining it with momentum, switches, cubes and turrets. The return value is a clean learning curve and the satisfaction of seeing space differently, so it is a campaign mastery model rather than a daily loop. Allworld can introduce city systems through small authored missions, then combine those rules into complex civic goals. The risk is limited replay after solutions are known. Sources: [GDCA archive](https://gamechoiceawards.com/archive-gdca-2008/), [Valve](https://www.valvesoftware.com/en/games/portal), [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/400/Portal/).

## Portal 2

Portal 2 won three BAFTAs in 2012, including Best Game. It expands linked portals with momentum, lasers, gels and a cooperative campaign requiring timing and communication. That social replay matters for Allworld: human and AI agents can coordinate distinct capabilities, share credit and revisit challenge missions with new pairings. The single-player learning curve remains useful for teaching city rules one mechanism at a time. Risks are partner availability and authored puzzles losing value once solved. This is campaign mastery plus social replay. Sources: [BAFTA winners release](https://www.bafta.org/media-centre/press-releases/game-british-academy-video-games-awards-winners-announced/), [Valve](https://www.valvesoftware.com/en/games/portal-2), [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/620/Portal_2/).

## Braid

Braid was the IGF 2006 Innovation in Game Design winner; the same official results name Darwinia, not Braid, the Visual Art winner. Its rewind system lets players undo movement and deaths, while each world changes how time interacts with space, shadows or parallel actions. Time-manipulation puzzles yield pieces that complete each world's picture and advance the journey. For Allworld, the useful transfer is safe rehearsal: let players explore consequences, then make districts obey different social or economic rules that must be learned. The risk is reducing stakes through over-reversibility and offering little replay after the authored breakthrough is found. This is mastery puzzle progression. Sources: [official IGF results in Game Developer, May 2006](https://media.gdcvault.com/GD_Mag_Archives/GDM_May_2006.pdf), [official site](https://braid-game.com/), [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/499180/Braid_Anniversary_Edition/).

## Inside

Inside won BAFTA 2017 Game Design. Its stealth platforming, possession mechanic and environmental storytelling make observation and timing the core skills; the world communicates through set pieces rather than exposition. The return impulse is campaign escalation and mystery resolution, not repeatable live content. Allworld can use compact missions where players observe a city, infiltrate or intervene, and infer systems from consequences. The risk is a short linear campaign with narrow thematic fit. This is campaign return. Sources: [BAFTA Game Design](https://www.bafta.org/awards/games/game-design/), [Playdead](https://playdead.com/games/inside/), [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/304430/INSIDE/).
