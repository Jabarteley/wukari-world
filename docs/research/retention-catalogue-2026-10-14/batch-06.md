# Retention catalogue batch 06

Sixteen verified action/adventure worlds were checked against official award-organizer or first-party award records, then paired with official game/developer pages for mechanics. These are design-transfer hypotheses for an Allworld social-life world, not claims that any mechanic caused commercial retention. Campaign continuation, replay mastery, exploration, collection and ongoing social play are kept distinct.

## Red Dead Redemption 2

**Award:** The Game Awards 2018, Best Narrative ([organizer video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XA3U977ppAY)). **Mechanics:** open-world riding/hunting/trading; camp relationships; honor and choice ([Rockstar](https://www.rockstargames.com/reddeadredemption2)). Deliberate wandering, optional stories and camp threads make return sessions legible, while Red Dead Online is a separate ongoing layer. Allworld can transfer this through resident memory, visible homes/work sites, social consequences and collectible histories. Risk: slow pacing and violence-heavy framing. Retention type: campaign continuation, exploration and collection completion.

## Grand Theft Auto V

**Award:** VGX 2013, Game of the Year ([Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2013/12/08/games-xbox-awards-vgx-2013/)). **Mechanics:** three switchable protagonists; heist preparation and crew choices; open-city activities and property ownership ([Rockstar](https://www.rockstargames.com/gta-v)). Switching roles and planning scores support replay, while GTA Online supplies an ongoing social sandbox beyond the campaign. Allworld can use complementary resident roles, AI/human crews, persistent jobs and civic reputation. Risk: crime satire may not fit every audience. Retention type: campaign replay plus ongoing social sandbox.

## The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild

**Award:** The Game Awards 2017, Game of the Year, reported by Nintendo on 8 December after the ceremony ([Nintendo](https://www.nintendo.com/en-za/News/2017/December/Bayonetta-3-The-Legend-of-Zelda-DLC-news-revealed-during-The-Game-Awards-1316327.html)). **Mechanics:** climbing/gliding/cooking/object physics; shrine puzzles; weather, temperature and equipment ([Nintendo](https://www.nintendo.com/us/games/detail/the-legend-of-zelda-breath-of-the-wild-switch/)). Curiosity creates return goals without daily obligation. Allworld can make rooftops, weather, tools and local knowledge open multiple city routes, with discoveries becoming map notes and civic improvements. Risk: systemic freedom can weaken authored focus. Retention type: exploration, mastery and collection completion.

## The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom

**Award:** Japan Game Awards 2024, Grand Award / Award for Excellence / Best Sales Award ([CESA](https://awards.cesa.or.jp/2024/en/year/prize/01.html)). **Mechanics:** Ultrahand construction; sky/surface/Depths layers; Fuse and Ascend ([Nintendo](https://www.nintendo.com/us/games/detail/the-legend-of-zelda-tears-of-the-kingdom-switch/)). Construction creates project-based mastery as players redesign vehicles and revisit puzzles. Allworld can let residents co-build homes, transit and businesses, preserving blueprints and community use as visible accomplishments. Risk: tool depth can overwhelm or bypass social goals. Retention type: creative mastery and exploration.

## God of War (2018)

**Award:** 22nd Annual D.I.C.E. Awards 2019, Game of the Year ([AIAS](https://www.interactive.org/awards/2019_22nd_awards.asp)). **Mechanics:** Leviathan Axe throw/recall; Atreus companion combat; combat, exploration and optional favors across Norse realms ([PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/god-of-war/)). Return is relational and mastery-led: optional favors, upgraded builds and a shared journey change how spaces read. Allworld can make companions remember promises, grow skills and display relationship history in homes. Risk: linear emotional structure limits simulation. Retention type: campaign continuation and relationship progression.

## God of War Ragnarök

**Award:** 26th Annual D.I.C.E. Awards 2023, Adventure Game of the Year ([AIAS](https://www.interactive.org/news/26th_annual_dice_awards_winners.asp)). **Mechanics:** realm-hopping exploration; weapon/skill builds; character relationship quests ([PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/god-of-war-ragnarok/)). Optional favors, post-campaign exploration and build experimentation extend the authored story. Allworld can connect city districts through resident-led favors, evolving skills and remembered family ties that alter conversations and spaces. Risk: content scale and localization cost. Retention type: campaign continuation, side-quest completion and mastery.

## The Last of Us (2013)

**Award:** British Academy Games Awards 2014, Best Game ([BAFTA winners release](https://www.bafta.org/media-centre/press-releases/british-academy-games-awards-in-2014-winners-announced/)). **Mechanics:** stealth and scarce resources; companion traversal; environmental storytelling ([PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/the-last-of-us-remastered/)). Its return pattern is campaign continuation and replay for higher difficulty, alternate stealth routes and emotional re-reading, rather than daily service. Allworld can use scarce resources, trusted companions and environmental clues to make neighborhoods feel inhabited. Risk: survival-horror intensity may exhaust players. Retention type: campaign continuation and replay mastery.

## The Last of Us Part II

**Award:** British Academy Games Awards 2021, EE Game of the Year ([BAFTA winners release](https://www.bafta.org/media-centre/press-releases/winners-announced-british-academy-games-awards-2021/)). **Mechanics:** stealth sandbox with sound/prone movement; crafting and skill upgrades; perspective shift across playable characters ([PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/the-last-of-us-part-ii/)). Return comes from alternate combat approaches, collectibles and replay that recontextualizes relationships. Allworld can let residents hold competing memories of the same city event, changing what each player can discover. Risk: perspective reversals need clear causality. Retention type: campaign continuation, replay mastery and collection.

## Uncharted 4: A Thief's End

**Award:** British Academy Games Awards 2017, Best Game ([BAFTA winners release](https://www.bafta.org/media-centre/press-releases/winners-announced-for-the-british-academy-games-awards-in-2017/)). **Mechanics:** climbing/rope/driving/swimming traversal; cover shooting; companion banter ([PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/uncharted-4-a-thiefs-end/)). Replay is driven by missed treasures, speed and difficulty after a polished authored trip. Allworld can create expeditions where resident companions provide banter and context, and discoveries become maps, museum pieces and local reputation. Risk: bespoke set pieces are costly and less reusable than systemic simulation. Retention type: campaign continuation and collection completion.

## Ghost of Tsushima

**Award:** British Academy Games Awards 2021, Audio Achievement ([BAFTA winners release](https://www.bafta.org/media-centre/press-releases/winners-announced-british-academy-games-awards-2021/)). **Mechanics:** stance-based swordplay; guiding wind, foxes and birds; charms, upgrades and cosmetics ([PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/ghost-of-tsushima/)). Exploration, duels and visual collections invite revisits while the story remains finite. Allworld can use sound, weather and resident signals to guide culturally grounded neighborhood exploration, with clothing, homes and landmarks recording earned status. Risk: historical context must not become decoration. Retention type: exploration, combat mastery and collection.

## Horizon Zero Dawn

**Award:** 21st Annual D.I.C.E. Awards 2018, Outstanding Technical Achievement ([AIAS](https://www.interactive.org/awards/2018_21st_annual_dice_awards.asp?tab=cat)). **Mechanics:** scan machine weaknesses and dismantle parts; open-world quests and settlements; skills and weapon modifications ([PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/horizon-zero-dawn/)). The scan-plan-execute hunt loop builds mastery, while new machines and lore sites support later expeditions. Allworld can give districts resource ecologies and residents tools, jobs and vulnerabilities that improve mission planning and civic care. Risk: combat and lore volume can make onboarding heavy. Retention type: mastery, exploration and collection completion.

## Death Stranding

**Award:** 23rd Annual D.I.C.E. Awards 2020, Outstanding Technical Achievement ([AIAS](https://www.interactive.org/awards/2020_23rd_annual_dice_awards.asp)). **Mechanics:** delivery route planning; asynchronous roads/bridges/signs/generators; connection-network expansion ([PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/death-stranding/)). Route optimization and helping strangers indirectly create continuing social purpose around an authored campaign. Allworld can make player-built paths, shelters and services useful to AI residents and other players, with attribution and maintenance forming visible civic legacy. Risk: consent, moderation and anti-griefing are essential. Retention type: campaign continuation plus asynchronous social contribution.

## Control

**Award:** 23rd Annual D.I.C.E. Awards 2020, Action Game of the Year ([AIAS](https://www.interactive.org/awards/2020_23rd_annual_dice_awards.asp?tab=pub)). **Mechanics:** telekinetic combat and destructible spaces; Metroidvania investigation; weapon and personal mod builds ([Remedy](https://www.remedygames.com/games/control/)). Players return to test powers, clear optional alerts and revisit spaces with new abilities. Allworld can use mutable buildings, resident investigations and upgrades that reveal new social and career paths. Risk: surreal ambiguity needs clear wayfinding. Retention type: mastery, investigation and optional mission completion.

## Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice

**Award:** Japan Game Awards 2019, Award for Excellence (Games of the Year Division) ([CESA](https://awards.cesa.or.jp/2019/en/prize/year/06.html)). **Mechanics:** posture-based deflection; grappling and vertical traversal; resurrection and prosthetic tools ([FromSoftware](https://www.sekirothegame.com/)). Return is deliberate practice: retry bosses, learn timing and pursue alternate endings, not daily-service behavior. Allworld can offer skill schools, visible mastery badges and mentor relationships that change how missions are solved. Risk: difficulty can exclude broad audiences without accessibility tuning. Retention type: replay mastery and campaign completion.

## Dark Souls III

**Award:** Japan Game Awards 2016, Award for Excellence (Games of the Year Division) ([CESA](https://awards.cesa.or.jp/2016/en/prize/year/05.html)). **Mechanics:** recover-or-lose soul drops; stats, weapons, armor and spells; interconnected shortcuts and hidden areas ([Dark Souls](https://www.darksouls.jp/)). Players return to improve routes, recover resources, try builds and complete optional areas, with community knowledge making discoveries shareable. Allworld can make city mastery legible through maps, shortcuts, mentors and recoverable setbacks. Risk: opaque punishment can feel hostile. Retention type: mastery, exploration and build experimentation.

## Bloodborne

**Award:** British Academy Games Awards 2016, Game Design ([BAFTA winners release](https://www.bafta.org/media-centre/press-releases/british-academy-games-awards-winners-announced/)). **Mechanics:** aggressive rally combat; transforming trick weapons; layered gothic investigation with shortcuts and chalice dungeons ([PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/bloodborne/)). Learning enemy patterns, refining weapon timing and uncovering optional layers drives return. Allworld can translate rally into social recovery, with transformed tools and hidden civic layers rewarding revisits. Risk: horror and opaque quests need audience controls and optional guidance. Retention type: mastery, investigation and optional dungeon completion.
