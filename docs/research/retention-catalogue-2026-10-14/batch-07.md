Award evidence is from the award organizer or an official developer/platform source; mechanics evidence is from official product or developer pages. “Return reason” and transfer notes are design inferences, not causal claims or private retention statistics.

## Allworld transfer and risk

The strongest transfers are shared-role work (It Takes Two, A Way Out, Overcooked and Keep Talking), short social sessions (Mario Kart 8 and Wii Sports), and layered city careers (Forza, Gran Turismo and Smash). Campaign platformers mainly transfer their discovery and collection patterns into districts, landmarks and resident stories rather than promising ongoing retention. Risks include partner dependence, latency, moderation, accessibility, content cost, live-service pressure and the danger of turning a clear mini-game into a shallow copy.
