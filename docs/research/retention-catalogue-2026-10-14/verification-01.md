# Award evidence audit — batches 01–03

Audit date: 2026-10-14. Scope: all 48 supplied game records in `batch-01.json` through `batch-03.json`. A record is verified only when an award organizer or the game's developer/publisher page identifies the title and exact year/category. Nominee lists, editorial roundups, reader-poll lower placements, and showcase prizes are not silently treated as equivalent post-release major-award wins.

## Result

- 42 post-release award wins verified.
- 1 Google Play Japan category honor verified and labelled as a category honoree rather than the later grand prize: Clash Royale.
- 2 pre-release showcase wins verified and labelled separately: Rainbow Six Siege and Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle.
- 3 supplied claims deferred: Brawl Stars, Alto's Adventure, and Stardew Valley.

That yields 45 records with exact verified recognition and 3 deferred records across the 48 supplied titles. For a strict catalogue of post-release award winners, count 42 from these three batches.

## Corrections with material effect

- Brawl Stars was a false 2019 Google Play US Users' Choice claim. Call of Duty: Mobile won.
- Monument Valley 2 was not Apple's 2017 game of the year. It is now tied to its verified The Game Awards 2017 Best Mobile Game win.
- Alto's Odyssey was not Apple's 2018 game of the year. It is now tied to its 2018 Apple Design Award.
- Florence moved from an uncertain Apple Design Award claim to its verified 2019 BAFTA Mobile Game win.
- The Room was a 2013 BAFTA Mobile & Handheld nominee. Its verified 2013 win was British Game.
- Alto's Adventure remains useful research but is now labelled an editorial Best of 2015 selection and excluded from the win count.
- Stardew Valley's Bronze reader-poll placement is recorded accurately and excluded from the win count.
- Sea of Thieves, Final Fantasy XIV Online, Animal Crossing: New Horizons, and The Sims were all confirmed in their originally named categories/years despite the initial suspicions.

## Mechanics citation cleanup

The Sims and The Sims 2 mechanics point to current official EA Legacy Collection pages rather than award pages; the JSON now states explicitly that these are modern re-release pages carrying the original games' core designs forward. Original Overwatch now uses only a 2017 Competitive Play source, with Overwatch 2-era Battle Pass and live-season evidence removed. CS:GO now uses Valve's archived CS:GO overview and Operation Shattered Web page; Steam app 730 is excluded because it now resolves to Counter-Strike 2. Destiny is labelled Destiny 1 and historical/legacy because Bungie states that it no longer receives planned updates or content. Civilization IV and Civilization V mechanics were narrowed to the resource-planning definition actually present on their award-category pages; unsupported detailed empire-system claims were removed. Genshin Impact's cadence claim was narrowed to the Original Resin regeneration and spend loop documented in HoYoWiki. All return reasons remain design inference rather than measured retention.

## Retrieval limit

The Game Awards' legacy `rewind/year-*` URLs currently redirect live requests to the organizer's homepage, although the edition content remains indexed under those organizer paths. Those records are preserved with exact edition URLs, and the limitation is called out here instead of presenting the pages as currently stable. Dota 2 likewise relies on Valve's official award badge because the IGN organizer archive was unavailable.
