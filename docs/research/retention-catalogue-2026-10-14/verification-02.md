# Verification 02 — batches 04–06

Verification date: 2026-10-14. Scope: 48 catalogue entries across batches 04, 05 and 06. Each award was checked against an award organizer's winner archive or an official developer/publisher record that explicitly names the title, year or edition, category and win. A nomination, finalist list or page that merely mentions the game was not accepted as win evidence.

## Result

- 48 of 48 selected entries now have an explicit verified award win.
- 0 selected entries remain award-unverified.
- 4 award identities were corrected because the draft had assigned another game's BAFTA Best Game win.
- 30 award URLs were replaced or upgraded to explicit winner records.
- 8 mechanic labels or descriptions were narrowed to match the cited first-party material.
- 6 reserve titles remain outside the verified set and are not counted.

## Disputed records resolved

- **Braid:** retained as the 2006 IGF Innovation in Game Design winner. The [official May 2006 Game Developer results](https://media.gdcvault.com/GD_Mag_Archives/GDM_May_2006.pdf) explicitly give Braid the design award and Darwinia the Visual Art award. The earlier finalist announcement was insufficient and has been replaced.
- **The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild:** retained as The Game Awards 2017 Game of the Year, but the November Nintendo page was replaced with Nintendo's [8 December 2017 ceremony recap](https://www.nintendo.com/en-za/News/2017/December/Bayonetta-3-The-Legend-of-Zelda-DLC-news-revealed-during-The-Game-Awards-1316327.html).
- **Ghost of Tsushima:** retained. BAFTA's [2021 winners release](https://www.bafta.org/media-centre/press-releases/winners-announced-british-academy-games-awards-2021/) explicitly names it the Audio Achievement winner.
- **Bloodborne:** retained. BAFTA's [2016 winners release](https://www.bafta.org/media-centre/press-releases/british-academy-games-awards-winners-announced/) explicitly names it the Game Design winner.

## Mechanic evidence corrections

- Returnal: replaced “persistent artifacts” with persistent discoveries; artifacts do not generally survive a cycle, while some equipment, traversal abilities and narrative discoveries do.
- Braid: removed “optional” and “unlock later worlds” from the puzzle-piece claim; the cited pages support completing pictures and advancing the journey.
- Mass Effect 2: replaced the vague “mission preparation” claim with squad recruitment and preparation for the suicide mission.
- Monster Hunter: World: renamed the cited loop “cooperative hunting” and removed crafting from the directly sourced mechanic description.
- Final Fantasy VII Remake: replaced “Midgar social hub” with the supported Midgar story setting and Avalanche missions.
- NieR: Automata: narrowed “genre-shifting play” to the official developer page's supported action-RPG combat description.
- God of War (2018): replaced the overly specific hub claim with combat, exploration and optional favors across Norse realms.
- Ghost of Tsushima: removed the duplicated stance label from the third mechanic and kept supported charms and cosmetic collection.

## Remaining gaps

No award-evidence gap remains among the 48 selected games. The six reserve entries in the batch files are intentionally unverified placeholders and remain excluded from the verified count. Return reasons and Allworld transfers remain design inferences, not causal retention claims.
