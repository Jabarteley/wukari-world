# Award and mechanics verification 03

Scope: batches 07–09. Every retained title was checked for an explicit winner statement from the award organizer or an official developer/publisher source. Nominee-only evidence was rejected. The JSON records carry `award_verified` and a short `award_evidence` paraphrase.

## Result

- Batch 07: 16 verified winners out of 16 retained titles.
- Batch 08: 16 verified winners out of 16 retained titles.
- Batch 09: 16 verified winners out of 16 retained titles.
- Unit total: 48 verified winners out of 48 retained titles.
- Unresolved award claims: 0.
- Current nine-batch aggregate at verification time: 141 verified winners out of 144 retained titles, with 3 explicitly rejected claims; the 120-game minimum is satisfied.

## Corrections

- Overcooked 2 was only a nominee for BAFTA Family in 2019. Its record now uses its explicit 2018 The Game Awards Best Family Game win from Team17.
- Chicory: A Colorful Tale and TOEM were restored from BAFTA’s 2022 Family and Debut Game winner records.
- A Short Hike was restored from GDC’s explicit 2020 IGF Seumas McNally Grand Prize announcement.
- Spiritfarer now points to the Canadian Game Awards organizer archive, which lists it as the 2021 Best Indie Game winner.
- The malformed 2015 Game Awards URLs for Her Story and Life Is Strange were corrected.
- Kentucky Route Zero was corrected to the exact BAFTA-winning edition, Kentucky Route Zero: TV Edition.
- Halo 3 is explicitly labeled as a Best of E3 2007 pre-release showcase winner.
- Forza Horizon 4, Halo 3, Halo: Reach, Call of Duty 4, Battlefield 3 and Left 4 Dead are labeled as historical or replay patterns rather than asserted as currently operated live services.
- Mechanics evidence was tightened for Journey, Celeste, Night in the Woods, Kentucky Route Zero, Before Your Eyes, Super Mario Galaxy, Forza Horizon 4, Wii Sports, Half-Life 2, Beat Saber and the original Resident Evil 4; unsupported or edition-mismatched specifics were removed or redirected to official sources.

## Source boundary

Organizer and official developer/publisher records were accepted when they explicitly named the game, category and result year. Editorial lists, sales ranks and nominee pages were not treated as wins. Halo 3 is retained only with its pre-release showcase label. Mechanics descriptions are conservative paraphrases of the cited official pages; the return and Allworld-transfer fields remain design analysis rather than measured retention evidence.
