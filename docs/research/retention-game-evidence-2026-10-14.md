# Retention game mechanics: first-party evidence for Allworld

Observed 2026-10-14. This is a mechanics reference, not a claim that any one feature caused a game's commercial success. Sources are first-party publisher, developer, or official support/community pages. “Documented” describes what the source states. “Inference” is a product translation for Allworld and should be validated in the actual player path.

## Whiteout Survival

### Documented mechanisms

1. **Daily mission requirements attached to recurring events.** Century Games’ March 2026 update says Wild Brawl became recurring, gives six attempts per session, and lowered the daily mission requirement to match the new cadence while keeping total event rewards unchanged. [Official update](https://www.centurygames.com/whiteout-survival-new-content-update/) (published 2026-03-04; observed 2026-10-14).

2. **Longer event tracks with staged missions and rewards.** The second-anniversary event describes “Chiefs of the Dawn” as seven stages where completing missions earns points and rewards. The same page describes completing designated missions on axes to earn a stage chest, with a final reliquary for completing all missions. [Official anniversary event](https://www.centurygames.com/whiteout-survival-2nd-anniversary/) (published 2025-03-05; observed 2026-10-14).

3. **Progression-gated mission sets.** The March 2026 update says Daybreak Island has missions with rewards and that “Blessing of Daylight” missions unlock once the Tree of Life is maxed; Prosperity targets unlock rewards including an avatar frame and name card. [Official update](https://www.centurygames.com/whiteout-survival-new-content-update/) (published 2026-03-04; observed 2026-10-14).

4. **Escalating reward quality as the shared world progresses.** Century Games’ September 2024 update says event rewards for many events improve as the State progresses. [Official update](https://www.centurygames.com/whiteout-survival-update/) (published 2024-09-04; observed 2026-10-14).

5. **Personal achievement record and visible prestige.** The September 2024 update introduced Dawn Honor, described as recording personal achievements in the Frostdragon Tyrant event. The 2025 anniversary event also awards a permanent skin to the top ten personal scorers and uses alliance and personal leaderboards. [Dawn Honor update](https://www.centurygames.com/whiteout-survival-update/) (published 2024-09-04; observed 2026-10-14); [anniversary event](https://www.centurygames.com/whiteout-survival-2nd-anniversary/) (published 2025-03-05; observed 2026-10-14).

### Transfer to Allworld

Use a small daily mission set tied to existing actions (visit a venue, join a table, help a resident, complete a street activity), then a weekly or event track with visible stages and a final cosmetic or status reward. Gate new mission families behind meaningful world progress, such as discovering a district or completing a resident arc. Let rewards grow with city or season maturity so a returning player has a reason to re-enter a live world. Personal records and non-transferable frames can make progress legible without turning the economy into a race for cash.

### Downsides and cautions

Daily requirements can turn play into checklist compliance and punish players who have limited time. Progression gates can make late-arriving players feel locked out. State-wide reward scaling risks widening the gap between established and new players. Leaderboards and top-ten rewards can motivate competition but may make ordinary players feel that their effort is invisible. Exact daily activity chest thresholds were not confirmed by an official source in this pass; the 40/80/120/160/215/270/325 sequence supplied separately by screenshot evidence remains screenshot evidence, not publisher-documented evidence.

## Candy Crush Saga

### Documented mechanisms

1. **First-try win streak with staged booster bags.** King’s Community Manager described the Candy Necklace: win new levels on the first try to unlock goodie bags for the next level; future rewards are shown; the maximum reward upgrade is reached after five consecutive wins, while the counter can continue. [King Community announcement](https://community.king.com/en/candy-crush-saga/discussion/comment/1211925/) (published 2020-10-06; observed 2026-10-14).

2. **Failure has an explicit streak decision.** The same announcement says a player who loses a level while on a streak can continue the level to keep the streak or leave and lose the streak and associated rewards. This makes the risk state visible at the point of failure. [King Community announcement](https://community.king.com/en/candy-crush-saga/discussion/comment/1211925/) (published 2020-10-06; observed 2026-10-14).

3. **Daily Treat Machine with six escalating five-day tiers.** King’s current help article documents six tiers, each representing a five-day login streak. The daily reward and an additional bonus grow by tier; day 30 is the final tier and adds a chance at a jackpot. Missing a day moves the meter to the previous tier and can reset it to day 1. This is a current help-desk description, while the older Candy Necklace announcement above is explicitly a 2020 feature/version description. [King Help: Daily Treat Machine](https://candycrush.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4402467400849--Daily-Treat-Machine) (observed 2026-10-14).

4. **Win streaks can feed competitive events.** King Community Manager QueenB’s feature announcement for Jelly Streak describes collecting jellies by winning levels, earning extra jellies for first-try wins, maintaining a streak for maximum jellies, and competing in groups of 15 where the top three receive rewards. It explicitly says the feature was being tested, so this is a staff-authored historical/test description rather than a universal current contract. [King Community Manager announcement](https://community.king.com/en/candy-crush-saga/discussion/comment/3124959) (observed 2026-10-14).

5. **Episode Race turns ordinary level progress into a competition.** King’s help article says players race through an Episode against other players; the fastest completion can win a bonus bundle of boosters and gold bars. It also documents that rewards can change during a Caramel Cup, so exact rewards are event/version-specific. [King Help: Episode Race](https://candycrush.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001846038--Episode-Race) (observed 2026-10-14).

### Transfer to Allworld

Apply streaks to actions where “first try” means a clean completion, such as finishing a resident request without abandoning it, winning a table round, or completing a short route. Show the next one to three rewards before the player acts. At failure, offer a clearly bounded recovery choice (continue, spend a scarce recovery token, or accept the reset). A weekly group challenge could turn individual activity into a small social contest, but should reward broad participation rather than only the top three.

### Downsides and cautions

Streak loss can feel punitive, especially when failure comes from connectivity or an unclear rule. A recovery purchase can become coercive if the player has already invested time. Daily login rewards privilege calendar availability over meaningful play. The current help article documents a drop to the previous Treat Machine tier, with a possible full reset to day 1; Allworld should offer a humane catch-up rule instead of copying that loss. Staff-authored community descriptions also indicate feature tests and variant rollout, so Allworld should avoid promising a universal mechanic before instrumentation confirms it works for all accounts.

## Clash of Clans

### Documented mechanisms

1. **Daily Star Bonus with a clear completion threshold and timer.** Supercell support says five stars from eligible attacks completes the Star Bonus, starts a 24-hour timer, and allows up to two bonuses to stack if the earlier one was left incomplete. Rewards include Gold, Elixir, Dark Elixir, and Ores; higher leagues improve the reward. [Supercell support: Loot and Star Bonus](https://support.supercell.com/clash-of-clans/en/articles/about-multiplayer-and-trophies-2.html) (observed 2026-10-14).

2. **Season challenge reward road.** Supercell release notes describe Starter Challenges with a pass of up to 5,000 points and Season Challenge changes, including auto-claiming unlocked rewards at season end. A later 2026 update says Season Challenges now start at Town Hall 3 for new players and rewards scale to Town Hall level. [TH14 release notes](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashofclans/blog/release-notes/full-patch-notes-th14-update/) (published 2021-04-12; observed 2026-10-14); [August 2026 update](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashofclans/blog/release-notes/august-update-3/) (published 2026-08-30; observed 2026-10-14).

3. **Clan Games turn individual challenges into shared reward tiers.** Clan members complete challenges and collect points for the clan; new tiers unlock for the whole clan. Eligibility requires at least one challenge, and maxing an individual point assignment grants an extra reward choice. Uncollected rewards expire after seven days. [Supercell support: Clan Games Rewards](https://support.supercell.com/clash-of-clans/en/articles/clan-games-rewards.html) (observed 2026-10-14).

4. **Magic items convert progression milestones into time savings.** Supercell documents items that instantly finish troop, spell, or building upgrades, boost collectors, or temporarily raise army power. They can be earned from Clan Games and Season Challenge reward tiers, events, or other shops, and the inventory has limits. [Supercell support: Magic Items and the Trader](https://support.supercell.com/clash-of-clans/en/articles/magic-items-and-the-trader.html) (observed 2026-10-14).

5. **Achievements and persistent social reputation.** Supercell support lists completing achievements as a way to earn Gems. Clan XP is gained through Wars and Clan Games; more stars and wins produce more XP and unlock clan perks. Clan Capital also records Reputation from contributions. [Free Gems and Resources](https://support.supercell.com/clash-of-clans/en/articles/how-can-i-get-free-gems-and-resources.html) (observed 2026-10-14); [Clan Score and XP](https://support.supercell.com/clash-of-clans/en/articles/clan-score-and-xp.html) (observed 2026-10-14); [Clan Capital release notes](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashofclans/blog/release-notes/clancapital-releasenotes/) (published 2022-03-28; observed 2026-10-14).

### Transfer to Allworld

A good Allworld analogue is one daily “activity meter” with a legible threshold, a timer, and a catch-up rule; a seasonal reward road with rewards scaled to a player’s stage; and a collective district or neighbourhood meter where one small contribution makes the player eligible for shared rewards. Use time-saving or access-expanding rewards sparingly: a shortcut, venue token, or cosmetic can feel valuable without destabilizing the economy. Persist achievements and contribution reputation separately from leaderboard rank.

### Downsides and cautions

Timers and stack limits can create anxiety or encourage low-value actions solely to avoid wasting a bonus. Clan-wide rewards can produce social pressure and unequal contribution. Auto-claim and seven-day expiry reduce lost rewards but can also hide the moment of choice. Instant progression items risk making ordinary progression feel slow or making paying players visibly dominant; Allworld should prefer bounded convenience over power.

## Fortnite

### Documented mechanisms

1. **Daily, weekly, seasonal, and milestone quest layers.** Epic support says Daily quests reset each day and Weekly quests refresh each Tuesday. Epic’s season announcement describes Daily, Season, and Milestone quests, with milestone quests having multiple stages and bonus XP for completing set counts. [Epic support: How to earn XP](https://www.epicgames.com/help/c-34254770/c-33726977/xp-fortnite-a17775130) (observed 2026-10-14); [Chapter 3 Season 1 announcement](https://www.fortnite.com/news/whats-new-in-fortnite-battle-royale-chapter-3-season-1-flipped) (published 2021-12-05; observed 2026-10-14).

2. **Catch-up XP after absence.** Epic support says Supercharged XP activates automatically when a player has not played for a day or more and temporarily boosts XP rates. This is a documented return mechanic rather than a streak penalty. [Epic support: How to earn XP](https://www.epicgames.com/help/c-34254770/c-33726977/xp-fortnite-a17775130) (observed 2026-10-14).

3. **Quest rewards combine progression and cosmetics.** Epic says quests usually reward XP, level-ups, or cosmetic items. In Play Your Way, each quest awarded XP and completing specified totals unlocked themed cosmetics at 5, 7, 9, and 11 quests. [Epic support: How to complete quests](https://www.epicgames.com/help/c-34254770/c-33726977/how-to-complete-fortnite-quests-a23969068) (observed 2026-10-14); [Play Your Way quests](https://www.fortnite.com/news/complete-the-new-play-your-way-quests-in-fortnite-for-xp-and-more-rewards) (published 2022-08-30; observed 2026-10-14).

4. **Cross-experience progression.** Epic support says XP advances all active passes simultaneously and quests exist across experiences including Rocket Racing, LEGO Fortnite, and Fortnite Festival. This lets one account’s activity contribute to several progression tracks. [Epic support: How to earn XP](https://www.epicgames.com/help/c-34254770/c-33726977/xp-fortnite-a17775130) (observed 2026-10-14); [How to complete quests](https://www.epicgames.com/help/c-34254770/c-33726977/how-to-complete-fortnite-quests-a23969068) (observed 2026-10-14).

5. **Accolades make authored moments rewardable.** Epic describes Accolades as achievements or awards that grant XP, with examples such as first landing, first legendary pickup, rebooting a player, hunting wildlife, or dealing shotgun damage. Frequency affects award size: frequent accolades grant smaller XP and rarer ones larger XP after calibration. [Epic Accolade devices announcement](https://www.fortnite.com/news/introducing-accolade-devices-for-uefn) (published 2023-08-28; observed 2026-10-14).

6. **Bankable daily work in a daily-reward redesign.** In Save the World, Epic removed Daily Login Rewards and moved value into Daily Quests and Mission Alerts; players could bank up to three Daily Quests to catch up after missing days. This is a useful documented example of replacing passive login pressure with bankable activity. [Epic v25.10 Daily Reward changes](https://www.fortnite.com/news/changes-coming-to-fortnite-save-the-worlds-daily-reward-system-in-v25-10) (published 2023-06-19; observed 2026-10-14).

### Transfer to Allworld

Use three horizons: daily prompts for a short session, weekly goals for return planning, and seasonal milestones for longer identity-building. Make milestone stages visible and mix XP-like progression with cosmetics, titles, badges, and world access. Add catch-up or banking so a missed day does not erase a player’s social standing. Authored accolades fit Allworld well: first visit to a landmark, first help given to a resident, first successful table win, or a rare discovery can each award a small, calibrated recognition.

### Downsides and cautions

Too many quest layers can create a noisy obligations screen and pull players away from self-directed play. Cross-track progression can make rewards hard to understand. Accolade frequency calibration needs guardrails so a common action does not flood the economy while a rare action becomes impossible to discover. Catch-up boosts should restore opportunity, not make absence optimal. Seasonal cosmetics and badges should remain legible after the season so the player’s history is not erased.

## Cross-game synthesis for Allworld

The strongest transferable pattern is a bounded ladder: a low-friction daily action, a weekly or event sequence, and a longer milestone that persists. Pair each rung with a visible next reward, a meaningful but non-inflationary prize, and a recovery rule for missed time. Whiteout and Clash show how group progress can make individual actions matter; Candy Crush shows the emotional clarity of a visible streak and an explicit failure choice; Fortnite shows how multiple horizons and catch-up can coexist.

The evidence does not establish that these mechanics caused retention or commercial outcomes. It establishes that the publishers document them as live progression structures. Allworld should instrument completion, abandon, recovery, next-session return, social participation, and reward redemption before deciding which ladder to keep. Avoid shipping exact copied thresholds or punitive streak loss until local playtests show that the mechanic creates return intent without turning the world into a checklist.
