# Brief: what avatar and 3D worlds teach a social platform

Searches ran 18 October 2026 by a research helper; figures are secondary unless a filing is cited. None of the failures found died for lack of 3D polish; they died on retention and cold start, on safety or trust shocks, or on unit economics.

## Platforms

## Companions, photos, voice, friend data

- No platform publishes the share of activity outside its 3D client. Closest analogues: Zepeto's feed; Snap's Bitmoji, used by 200M+ people a day with about 70% of daily users linked (Snap, self-reported); Roblox Moments.
- Photos: Zepeto Photo Booth (above); VRChat photography reaches physical exhibitions (Fujifilm contest, Tokyo); GTA Snapmatic contests. Evidence is anecdotal; no sharing rates published.
- Voice: Clubhouse downloads peaked at 8.7M in June 2021 and fell about 95% by June 2022 (Appfigures); live-only, no replays, weak discovery, copied by Twitter, Spotify and Facebook; its 2023 layoff note said it had become harder to find friends and to fit long conversations into a day. Discord voice is claimed at 40%+ of user minutes (weak source) against 200M+ MAU.
- Friends: Facebook's "7 friends in 10 days" exists only in secondary sources. Roblox published no friends-versus-retention figure. Pew 2015: 78% of teen online gamers feel closer to existing friends, 36% of teens with online friends met them through games.

## Transferable lessons

1. Retention, not acquisition, is the problem: Bondee, Clubhouse and Horizon all had spikes without a habit.
2. The product must work with friends already inside; design onboarding to reach several real connections fast and measure your own number.
3. Empty worlds kill: concentrate people, show who is where before asking anyone to go, keep rooms warm.
4. Do not make the 3D client the front door (Horizon to mobile; Rec Room's overstretch).
5. Give non-3D people a native surface, not a lesser one (Zepeto feed, Roblox Moments and Chat, Bitmoji).
6. Let the avatar be the shared object between 2D and 3D.
7. Make avatar photos effortless and shareable with a link back in; measure the funnel.
8. Persistent groups beat transient rooms (VRChat Groups and Mutuals; Bondee's cap).
9. Voice needs persistence and a text fallback (Clubhouse).
10. Trust and safety can end the company (Habbo 2012, Bondee 2023): publish ownership and data practice early, moderate public rooms before growth.
11. Creator economies need unit economics before promises (Second Life 10% take and US$1.1B paid; Rec Room's costs).
12. Hangout is a product (Brookhaven, VRChat events), and hangouts need a way to hop onward.
13. Registered users are vanity; track day-7, day-30 and connections per user.
14. Lockdown-era growth fades (Zepeto, Clubhouse).

Not verified: outside-client activity share, hangout versus play time, Discord server-join retention, Roblox friend-count retention, the origin of the "10 days" window, the IMVU Next feed, Zepeto 2025 MAU, Gather's trend, Rec Room's 30c share, Clubhouse cohort percentages, Bondee MAU.
