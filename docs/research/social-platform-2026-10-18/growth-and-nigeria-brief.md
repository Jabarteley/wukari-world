# Brief: growth loops, Nigeria, content formats, safety law, money

Searches ran 18 October 2026 by a research helper. Tags: [P] primary or filing, [S] secondary, [U] unverified.

## Growth loops that built a graph from zero

1. Facebook "7 friends in 10 days": a correlation from retained versus churned users turned into a target; no primary document; Andrew Chen treats it as a rallying number. [S]
2. BeReal: one random daily window; 2022 surge helped by paid college ambassadors (reported US$30 a referral); about 41M downloads in 2022 (Apptopia); daily users fell from about 15M (Oct 2022) to about 6M (spring 2023); bought by Voodoo in 2024 for a reported EUR 500M. [S]
3. Locket: a home-screen widget of friends' photos, friends capped at 20; a TikTok tutorial took it to number one and 2M users in two weeks (TechCrunch 2022); claims 9M daily users in 2025 (TechCrunch, Nov 2025). [S/company]
4. Gas and tbh: school-based compliments polls; 1M daily users in two months, then stalled; Discord bought and shut it in 2023; a US$6.99 a week paid tier. [S]
5. Yope: private-group photos; Feb 2025 2.2M monthly and 800k daily users, 40% day-7 retention, 70 to 80% arriving by invite, average age 18 (TechCrunch). [S/company]
6. noplace: 500k waitlist from TikToks, number one in the US store in 2024; later path not found. [S]
7. Clubhouse: invite-only launch, about 10M installs in Feb 2021, installs down about 66% by end of April; opened to all July 2021; copied, and unrecorded rooms were hard to moderate. [S]
8. Partiful: about 500k monthly users Q1 2025, up 400% year on year (Sensor Tower via CNBC); guest-becomes-host is the evident loop. [S]
9. Strava: about 50M monthly users; clubs nearly quadrupled to 1M in 2025 (TechCrunch). [S]
10. Discord: 200 to 260M monthly users (estimates); about 90% of 32.6M servers have under 15 members (Sacra, Demandsage). [S]
11. TikTok For You barely depends on follower count; Instagram and Facebook now show much more unconnected content. [S/U]
12. LinkedIn address-book import feeding People You May Know is documented by LinkedIn Engineering; the profile-view loop is [U].
13. Wordle: the emoji grid shipped when about 90 people played daily (Nov 2021); the game reached 2M players within weeks (TechCrunch). [S]
14. Spotify Wrapped 2025: 200M users engaged on its first day (TechCrunch, Dec 2025). [S]

Survivors tie to a durable unit (servers, clubs, a close-friend widget) or a shareable artefact (the grid, Wrapped). Single-trick loops (timing window, scarcity, school polls, subsidised data) spiked and fell.

## Nigeria and Africa

- DataReportal Digital 2026: 109M internet users (45.5%), 165M mobile connections, median age 18.1. Ad reach: TikTok 47.8M, Facebook 38.0M, YouTube 30.5M, Snapchat 23.0M (+30%), Instagram 10.0M (-6%), X 6.95M (-11%). No WhatsApp or Telegram figure there. [P/S]
- WhatsApp: more than 95% of Nigerian internet users aged 16+ (Statista, Q3 2024); installed on 95% of urban smartphones (BrandsPur, Sept 2026). No Nigeria data on Status, Channels or Communities. [S]
- Devices: Android 82.7%, iOS 17.2% of mobile web (StatCounter, June 2026). GSMA: the barrier is device price, not coverage; an entry smartphone is about 76% of income for the poorest fifth in Sub-Saharan Africa (Nairametrics, Sept 2026). [S]
- Data: about NGN 800 for a typical 1 GB monthly bundle in 2026; average use 8.15 GB a month (Marketing Edge). [S]
- Culture: comedy skits dominate Nigerian TikTok; Afrobeats challenges drive music; GeoPoll reportedly found 66% of young Nigerians on TikTok. [S]
- Diaspora: remittances US$19.5 to 25B a year (World Bank 2023 US$19.5B). LinkedIn about 12.9M Nigerian users (NapoleonCat estimate). [S]
- African social apps: 2go rose on Java feature phones and failed to move to Android; Ayoba reached 35M+ users on MTN zero-rated data and was pulled from the stores on 20 March 2026 for weak retention against WhatsApp (TechCabal, ITWeb). Subsidised growth did not survive. [S]

## Safety and compliance

- Instagram teen defaults: under-18 private by default, DMs only from people they follow or are connected to (Sept 2024); under-16s cannot go Live without a parent; PG-13 content limits (Oct 2025). Meta says 97% of 13 to 15s kept the defaults (company claim). [S]
- UK Online Safety Act: illegal-content risk assessments due 16 March 2025, children's access assessment 16 April 2025, children's code from 25 July 2025; fines to 10% of global turnover; no small-service carve-out found (Reed Smith, RPC). [S]
- EU DSA: Article 19 exempts micro and small enterprises from the extra online-platform duties; hosting duties (notice and action, Art. 16; contact point, Art. 11) still apply. Check the Regulation text. [S]
- Nigeria: under the NDPA a child is under 18 and needs parental consent, with an internal inconsistency at s.31(5) that reads as 13 (ALN); GAID 2025 in force 19 Sept 2025 with registration for controllers of major importance and a 72-hour breach report (DLA Piper); the NITDA platform code (reported both as draft and in force) would require a local representative, a complaints channel, removal in 24 or 48 hours, and a Nigerian entity above 100k users (Mondaq). Get Nigerian counsel to confirm. [S]

## Monetisation

- Roblox Q2 2025: 111.8M DAU, 23.4M monthly payers, US$12.86 bookings per DAU (8-K); roughly a fifth pay, mixing a monthly and a daily count. [P]
- Discord Nitro: an estimated 5 to 8M subscribers against 200 to 260M monthly users (2 to 4%); about half of an estimated US$561M 2025 revenue (Sacra). [S/U]
- Games generally: 1 to 5% of users pay; 3 to 8% free-to-paid is called healthy for subscription apps. [S]
- Zepeto: creators earn after a 30% fee; no payer share found. Gas's US$6.99 a week tier deepened social pressure and is the pattern to avoid.

## Ten recommendations from the brief

1. Make the shareable image the primary growth surface, readable without 3D.
2. Build a small-group join, not a global graph; aim for several real connections in the first days and measure your own number.
3. Make host-created events first class with a plain page that previews in WhatsApp.
4. Seed a graph-free feed from auto-captured moments, AI residents and city events.
5. Design for WhatsApp: share to Status and groups, opt-in contact suggestions, voice notes.
6. Ship a text-and-image lite mode with a visible download size and no mandatory 3D.
7. Do not depend on telecom subsidies or scarcity.
8. Default minors to private, no DMs or voice from strangers; age assurance beyond a self-declared date.
9. Minimum compliance pack: report button, a recorded 24 to 48 hour moderation target, Nigerian counsel on NITDA and NDPC, a UK and EU risk-assessment note before marketing there.
10. Monetise cosmetics and creator payouts, not friend access; hold feed ads until the graph exists; expect a low single-digit payer share.
