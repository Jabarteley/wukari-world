# Worldwide missions, stories and journeys

## Coverage and content

The current supported catalogue contains 284 open, loadable cities across 243 country or territory identities. These are game-supported identities, not a sovereign-state count. Kaduna is open in the current city index despite an older reserved declaration. The [coverage report](coverage.json) records every city, its content references, and source hashes from the generator run.

Each city has two linked four-chapter stories, for 568 generated episodes plus the two existing Lagos episodes. The city seeds reuse actual game hosts, venues, spots, activities, and regulars. The stories are authored game fiction using shared narrative patterns; they are not 568 independently researched plots or biographies. Mementos identify the fictional nature of the host’s story. First Steps centers on a welcome and wayfinding; A Place Kept Open returns to the earlier choice and prepares an accessible handover. Work, Study, and Help are story approaches, not claims of a job or student credit.

Proofs are tied to the current city and exact configured activity, venue, spot, or NPC. A completed local activity can provide the host’s welcome note when the regular is away or asleep. Help also has an explicit activity alternative. Merely opening a page, repeating a choice, receiving an agent response, or arriving at a venue does not establish activity completion.

The 284 city and 243 country collections reuse permanent episode receipts; there is no second award ledger. The existing Akoka collection remains separate and accepts only its original keepsakes. Earned city and country history stays available after travel, while continuing an unfinished city story requires returning to that city.

Outside Lagos, the catalogue adds 849 daily and 849 weekly mission definitions: one life, discovery, and social option per non-Lagos city. The selector prefers an eligible local discovery. Existing generic missions remain available, and the three daily/weekly slots, v1 cash rewards, set bonuses, and swap rules remain shared. City-specific rows require their configured local proof; incomplete missions remain unfinished, and already-earned rewards remain claimable after travel. Calendar attendance is only eligible where the city has calendar content and, for daily event-dependent rows, an event is scheduled.

## Content maintenance

Run `npm run progression:check` before publishing city content changes. The production prebuild runs this deterministic check. `npm run progression:content` regenerates the catalogue and coverage from the actual supported city modules, validating host references and two distinct free, ungated activities at public, always-open venues.

When a city is added or its content changes, complete its city factory gates, regenerate the seeds and coverage, and review its story and mission destinations. Keep released IDs and reward meanings stable; an incompatible proof or narrative needs an explicit version and migration. The seed catalogue is data and type imports only. Full world episode content and mission registration belong to the lazy progression pack, keeping first-load content bounded.
