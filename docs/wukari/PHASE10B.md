## Research and rights

[Overture catalog](https://stac.overturemaps.org/catalog.json) reported release2026-10-21.0; collection metadata and intersecting per-file STAC bounds were inspected before bounded GeoParquet queries. The query uses one cloud mirror per asset, filters bbox, retains complete intersecting features and preserves exact WKB-derived WGS84 coordinates. Source record IDs, versions, confidence and licenses are available through the interactive feature inspector and [provenance inventory](../../data/wukari/phase10b/overture-provenance.json). Release collection/item extents are checked before this release-specific file-selection optimization; mismatches abort instead of silently accepting incomplete selection.

[Overture upstream terms](https://docs.overturemaps.org/attribution/) distinguish ODbL buildings/transportation/infrastructure/land-use, CC-BY4.0 ESA land cover and the actual CDLA-Permissive2.0 Meta/Overture place records. Google Open Buildings detections within Overture are explicitly open CC-BY4.0 data, distinct from protected Google Maps screenshots; they were not traced here. Source records with missing license fields resolve through documented provider terms, with zero unresolved providers in this bounded set. Overture lists Microsoft contributions under ODbL; the current direct [Microsoft dataset](https://github.com/microsoft/GlobalMLBuildingFootprints) documents CDLA-Permissive2.0. Both version/provenance chains remain separate, with the CDLA text included. ODbL source offer, required upstream attribution and editable derived databases: [data README](../../data/wukari/phase10b/README.md).

Microsoft's current index selected only Nigeria quadkey122221301, approximately7.4MB compressed, streamed once and spatially filtered; no country-wide footprint download. Duplicate review uses unchanged projected polygons and a spatial index: IoU>0.5, smaller-area overlap>0.7 or centroid distance<3m flags a probable duplicate; other positive intersections over0.5m² flag mismatches. These are review thresholds, not definitive building identities. Additional detections remain candidates. No footprint is shifted, resized, deleted from source records or automatically added to gameplay. The Google/OSM/Microsoft detections may share upstream imagery or import ancestry; source agreement is not a field survey.

Input is EPSG:4326 [longitude,latitude]. Display/metric review uses the existing project sphere6371008.8m, fixed standard parallel9N, x east/z south, origin at the candidate. Original source coordinates are preserved; no scaling to a screenshot or invented elevations. The windows differ slightly and full crossing features extend beyond them, so count differences alone do not measure dataset completeness.

## Firsthand gate measurement and acquisition request

In the app, Places → Review location opens the campus overview. Its GPS panel records only after Start is pressed, keeps samples locally, stops within90 seconds and exports a GeoJSON report only when requested. No upload, database write, map import or spawn approval occurs. Browser permission may be declined. Phones need a secure origin or a standalone GPS app; the laptop's localhost address is not a phone-accessible deployment.

1. At each entrance separately, stand stationary in a permitted area beside the entrance, away from traffic; select Main or Second and attest physical identification. Do not record a Google pin or coordinates while riding.
2. Collect at least ten readings with reported accuracy≤15m spanning30 seconds, preferably repeat on another visit/device. Record WGS84 longitude/latitude, UTC timestamp, reported accuracy, gate identity, observation position and any offset to the physical opening. Device-reported accuracy is not surveyed accuracy.
3. Export the raw samples/median report deliberately. A weak, short or unwitnessed record stays insufficient. Attach your own licensed photo of the gate and the approach, taken without exposing private individuals or security-sensitive detail.
4. Ask FUW Physical Planning or Works to corroborate gate identity and obtain a licensed georeferenced plan: GIS GeoPackage/GeoJSON preferred, or DXF/DWG/PDF with CRS/datum, units, scale, date/revision, control points and accuracy. Request gate points/opening widths, internal road/footpath centerlines and surveyed widths, barriers/walls, building footprints and official building IDs/names/functions, hostels/faculties/admin/lecture halls, landscaping/open spaces and elevation where available.
5. Obtain written permission for public redistribution, modification and commercial game use, required attribution and any excluded areas. A scan without coordinates needs at least three non-collinear surveyed controls plus independent checkpoints; do not substitute Google screenshot control points. A virtual pedestrian starting patch then needs swept clearance from source buildings/walls/road traffic and a connected walking route. Gate verification alone does not certify public access or physical safety.
