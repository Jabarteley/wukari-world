# Allworld native character notices — 16 October 2026

This is the selected game character release, not a standalone asset or motion library.

## Models and skirts

Selected bodies, hair, clothing, shoes and targets derive from official MakeHuman System Assets and retain their recorded CC0 terms. The selected round12 skirts use female_elegantsuit01 lower clothing and female_sportsuit01/elegant upper clothing, with locally authored pose morphs. The original lab's actual URL resolution is preserved.

The Franky mini-skirt files referenced by historical final-pass item metadata are overridden by the catalogue's body-specific round12 selection and are not shipped. Their conflicting CC0/AGPL source records remain research provenance, not a licence claim about selected assets.

Source inventory and selection explanation: https://github.com/kromate/joinallworld/tree/main/docs/character-assets
Official System Assets: https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html

## Motion

The selected iteration2-upper-palm animation includes locally authored adjustments and CMU subject07 trial02 walking / subject09 trial01 running, plus the CC0 Mesh2Motion greeting source. These terms are separate from the character meshes.

The data used in this project was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu. The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217.

CMU permits inclusion in commercial products but prohibits direct resale of motion data, including converted forms. The animations are included as part of Allworld, not distributed as a standalone motion library. Raw research motion files are not part of the browser package. https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/
