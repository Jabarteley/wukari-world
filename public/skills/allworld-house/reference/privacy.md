# What never goes into a house

A real house can show where a person lives. A house in Allworld is seen by the player's visitors, and a world by anyone it is open to.

- No house number, street name, estate name, landmark sign or company sign, on the walls or as a texture.
- No faces, people, photographs of people, car plates, letters, documents or screens with anything on them.
- Room names are generic: "Bedroom 2", "Kitchen", not "Ada's room".
- Projected photo textures: crop out anything above before using them; blur when in doubt.
- Keep the photos with the player. Do not upload them anywhere; only the finished house goes to the game.
